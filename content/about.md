# About Cityvora

Cityvora is a city discovery project built to make exploring a place feel simple, useful and inspiring.

We focus on the details that help people understand a destination: neighborhoods, landmarks, food, culture, stays, local experiences and practical trip ideas.

## What we publish

- City guides and destination overviews
- Places and neighborhoods worth exploring
- Food and cultural highlights
- Practical planning ideas
- Search-friendly reference content designed to be easy to scan

Cityvora is independent from the separate Tech Blog project and has its own website, content system and repository.
