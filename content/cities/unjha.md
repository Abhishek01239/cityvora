Unjha is a major trading town in Mehsana district, especially known for its agricultural-market economy and north Gujarat commercial life.

## What to See
- **Unjha market area** — the town's defining commercial district.
- **Vadnagar** — nearby for Kirti Toran and historic temples.
- **Modhera** — a short regional heritage trip.

## Food
Look around **Station Road and the main market** for Gujarati farsan, thali and breakfast. Try **fafda-jalebi, khaman, ganthiya and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** station/market area
- **Mid-range:** central Unjha
- **Higher-end:** Mehsana for a larger hotel selection

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,300/day budget; ₹2,300–₹4,500/day mid-range.
