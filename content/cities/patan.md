## Places to Explore
- **Rani Ki Vav** — UNESCO World Heritage stepwell.
- **Patan Patola heritage** — learn about the city's famous double-ikat weaving tradition.
- **Sahasralinga Talav** — historic water structure.
- **Khan Sarovar** — local lake and open space.
- **Modhera Sun Temple** — excellent nearby heritage excursion.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, khaman and local farsan around **Deri Road, Station Road and the old market**.

## Where to Stay
**Patan city centre and Station Road** are convenient for sightseeing.

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Rani Ki Vav → Patola heritage → old market.  
**Day 2:** Modhera Sun Temple → local Patan food.
