Lathi is a taluka headquarters in Amreli district, about 24 km east of Amreli. It is known for the Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir, the Kalapi Tirth museum dedicated to the poet Kalapi, and everyday Saurashtra town life.

## Places to Explore

- **Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir** - well-known Hanuman temple in Bhurakhiya village (Lathi taluka); major fair in the month of Chaitra.
- **Kalapi Tirth / Kalapi museum** - site preserving the memory and works of the Gujarati poet Kalapi, who belonged to Lathi.
- **Shri Swaminarayan Mandir** - important local temple.
- **Nani Bazaar** - local market for everyday shopping and traditional dress materials.
- **Nearby Narayan Sarovar (Dudhala area)** - quiet water body for a short outing.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town eateries and stalls:** Gujarati and Kathiyawadi thali, rotla, farsan, thepla and chai.
- **Bazaar area:** simple vegetarian snacks and meals; stall locations can change.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Lathi town:** Basic hotels and lodges.
- **Amreli (nearby):** Wider choice of rooms if you are touring the district.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding intercity travel:

- **Budget:** Rs 1,000-2,000
- **Mid-range:** Rs 2,500-4,500
- **Comfort:** Rs 5,000+
- **Food:** roughly Rs 250-1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** autos and shared vehicles within town and to nearby temples.

Hotel prices vary by date and festival periods (especially around the Bhurakhiya fair).

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive Lathi, visit Swaminarayan Mandir and Nani Bazaar, evening meal.

**Day 2:** Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir and Kalapi Tirth, then continue toward Amreli or other Saurashtra stops.

## Before You Go

- October to March is more comfortable for walking and temple visits.
- Confirm fair dates and temple timings locally if travelling in Chaitra.
- Carry cash for smaller shops and stalls.
