Lathi is a taluka headquarters in Amreli district, about 24 km east of Amreli. It is known for the Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir, the Kalapi Tirth linked to the poet Kalapi, and everyday Saurashtra market life.

## Places to Explore

- **Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir** - well-known Hanuman temple in Bhurakhiya (Lathi taluka); a large fair is held in the month of Chaitra.
- **Kalapi Tirth** - site associated with the Gujarati poet Kalapi, with a museum preserving his work.
- **Shri Swaminarayan Mandir** - important local temple.
- **Nani Bazaar** - local market lanes for everyday shopping.
- **Narayan Sarovar area (near Dudhala)** - quiet water body for a short outing.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Central bazaar / town eateries:** Gujarati thali, thepla, khakhra, farsan and everyday vegetarian meals.
- **Street stalls:** chai, snacks and seasonal items; stall locations shift, so ask locally.
- Typical Saurashtra-Gujarati flavours dominate; non-vegetarian options are limited in many small-town spots.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Town centre:** Convenient for the bazaar and temples.
- **Near main roads / bus stand area:** Practical for onward travel within Amreli district.

Options are mostly small hotels and guesthouses rather than large chains. Confirm current availability on booking platforms.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding intercity travel:

- **Budget:** Rs 1,000-2,000
- **Mid-range:** Rs 2,500-4,500
- **Comfort:** Rs 5,000+
- **Food:** roughly Rs 250-1,000/day depending on thali vs simpler meals.
- **Local transport:** autos and shared vehicles, typically Rs 100-400/day within town and nearby.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and explore the central bazaar, visit Swaminarayan Mandir, evening walk and Gujarati meal.

**Day 2:** Bhurakhiya Hanuman Mandir and Kalapi Tirth, then depart or continue toward Amreli.

## Before You Go

- October to March is more comfortable for walking.
- Summers are hot; start outdoor visits early.
- Carry cash for smaller shops and stalls.
- Confirm temple opening times and fair dates locally.
