## Places to Explore
- **Dolat Vilas Palace** — heritage architecture.
- **Hathmati River** — useful for a relaxed city outing.
- **Tower Chowk** — central local landmark and market area.
- **Darbari Vav** — historic stepwell.
- **Akodara** — nearby village and rural experience.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, gathiya and farsan around **Station Road, Tower Chowk and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Station Road and central Himatnagar** are convenient.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Tower Chowk → Hathmati → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Dolat Vilas → Darbari Vav → Akodara.
