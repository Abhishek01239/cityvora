Godhra is a major city in Panchmahal district and a regional commercial and transport centre in eastern Gujarat.

## What to See
- **Panchmahal region** — a good base for nearby heritage and nature trips.
- **Pavagadh–Champaner** — a major UNESCO World Heritage destination within the wider region.
- **Local markets and old-city areas** — for everyday food and shopping.

## Food
Try **Gujarati thali, dal-dhokli, khichdi-kadhi, fafda and jalebi** around **Tower Road and the central market**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** railway station/central market
- **Mid-range:** main-city roads
- **Higher-end:** hotels around the Godhra–Halol corridor

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,500/day budget; ₹2,500–₹5,000/day mid-range.
