## Places to Explore
- **Girnar Hill** — major pilgrimage and trekking destination.
- **Uparkot Fort** — historic fort with caves and stepwells.
- **Mahabat Maqbara** — ornate Indo-Islamic architecture.
- **Ashoka Rock Edicts** — ancient inscriptions.
- **Sakkarbaug Zoological Garden** — known for Asiatic lions.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi food, fafda-jalebi and local farsan around **Kalwa Chowk, Majevadi Gate and Talav Gate**.

## Where to Stay
**Kalwa Chowk, Talav Gate and Girnar Road** are practical bases.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Uparkot → Mahabat Maqbara → Ashoka Edicts.  
**Day 2:** Girnar area → Sakkarbaug → local food.
