Dehgam (also spelled Dahegam) is a municipality and taluka headquarters in Gandhinagar district, about 30 km from Gandhinagar and 40-45 km from Ahmedabad. It has roots going back centuries under successive dynasties and later Gaekwad administration; today it is a quiet agricultural and small-industry town with easy access to regional heritage sites.

## Places to Explore

- **Local temples** — including Hanumanji Temple and Dholeshwar Mahadev Temple, among other neighbourhood shrines.
- **Town centre** — everyday Gujarati market life and community spaces; statues of B.R. Ambedkar and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel are local landmarks.
- **Adalaj Stepwell** — major historic stepwell a short drive away (often combined with Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad itineraries).
- **Nal Sarovar Bird Sanctuary** — wetland birding destination within reasonable day-trip range (seasonal).
- **Nandol Dehegam railway station area** — on the Ahmedabad-Udaipur line, useful for rail travellers.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town eateries:** Gujarati thali, thepla, dhokla, farsan, kadhi-rice and seasonal undhiyu.
- **Highway / main-road dhabas:** simple vegetarian meals for travellers between Ahmedabad and the north-east of the district.
- Chai and snack stalls are common; prefer busy places for freshness.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Dehgam town:** Basic hotels and lodges.
- **Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad:** Far more options if you want modern hotels and plan day trips into Dehgam / Adalaj.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort (if based in Gandhinagar/Ahmedabad):** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** autos, buses or hired car for Adalaj / Nal Sarovar side trips.

Prices rise during major festivals and peak winter tourist weeks in the Ahmedabad region.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive Dehgam → local temples and town walk → evening meal.

**Day 2:** Morning trip to Adalaj Stepwell (and optionally continue toward Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad) or Nal Sarovar if in birding season.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable period for sightseeing.
- Adalaj and Nal Sarovar involve short road journeys; confirm timings and entry rules.
- Carry water and sun protection in warmer months.
- Combine easily with Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad stays.
