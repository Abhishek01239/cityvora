Chotila is a town and taluka headquarters in Surendranagar district, on the main road between Rajkot and Surendranagar. Travellers know it for the Chamunda Mata temple on Chotila hill, a stepped climb above the highway town. The temple is a working pilgrimage site rather than a museum, and festival days draw large crowds from Saurashtra. The town itself is a market and transport stop, not a resort.

## Places to Explore

- **Chamunda Mata temple** — the hill shrine above town. The usual visit is the stepped path up, darshan, and the view back over the highway plain. Go early; midday in summer is harsh on the steps.
- **Hill approach and town edge** — small shops for prasad, flowers and snacks at the base. Follow the marked public path rather than informal shortcuts.
- **Chotila market** — the working bazaar for daily goods, useful if you are breaking a Rajkot–Ahmedabad road trip.
- **Surendranagar** — the district town to the east, with a larger market and rail link if you need a bigger base.
- **Than and the wider Surendranagar circuit** — possible add-ons only with a vehicle. Do not treat them as a walk from the temple.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Temple base and highway stalls:** tea, fafda, ganthia, simple Gujarati snacks and thali meals aimed at pilgrims and bus passengers.
- **Town market:** everyday Kathiawadi vegetarian food, rotla, kadhi and dal. Choice is limited after dark.
- **Surendranagar:** more restaurants if you stay in the district town instead of Chotila.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Chotila:** a handful of lodges and highway stays. Enough for one night if booked, thin on choice.
- **Surendranagar:** better hotel range and the rail station for onward travel.
- **Rajkot:** the practical city base if Chotila is only a daytime temple stop on a Saurashtra loop.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,400–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+ if you stay in Rajkot and day-trip
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹800/day.
- **Local transport:** buses between Rajkot, Chotila and Surendranagar are inexpensive. A hired cab for a temple stop plus return is often ₹1,500–₹3,000 depending on the start city.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Road or bus from Rajkot or Ahmedabad → early climb to Chamunda temple → town market → overnight in Chotila or Surendranagar.

**Day 2:** Morning in Surendranagar market, or continue west to Rajkot and the Saurashtra circuit. Do not stack a long rann drive onto the same morning unless the vehicle is already arranged.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season for the hill steps. Navratri and other shrine festivals are crowded; shoes and bags follow temple rules.
- The climb is real. Carry water and skip the midday ascent in April–June.
- Photography inside the sanctum is often restricted. Ask at the gate.
- Highway traffic on the Rajkot–Surendranagar road is fast. Use official bus stops and crossings.
