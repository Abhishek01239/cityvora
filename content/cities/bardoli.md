Bardoli is a town in Surat district closely associated with the Bardoli Satyagraha and the region's agricultural and educational activity.

## Places to See

Sardar Patel National Museum, Swaraj Ashram, Kedareshwar Temple and local heritage sites.

## Food to Try

Gujarati farsan and thali around Station Road and the main market; try locho, khaman, fafda-jalebi and Gujarati thali.

## Where to Stay

Station Road and the main market are convenient for short visits.

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,400/day; mid-range ₹1,600–2,800/day; food ₹200–500/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
