## Places to Explore
- **Gandhidham Gate** — recognizable local landmark.
- **Gandhi Samadhi** — quiet heritage site.
- **Kandla Port area** — important regional commercial landmark.
- **Adipur** — nearby town with temples and local markets.
- Use Gandhidham as a base for wider **Kutch** trips.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Kutchi dabeli, Gujarati thali, farsan and sweets around **Tagore Road, Adipur and central Gandhidham**.

## Where to Stay
**Tagore Road, Adipur and central Gandhidham** are practical.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹150–₹700/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Gandhidham → Adipur → local food.  
**Day 2:** Kandla area → Gandhi Samadhi → begin a Kutch excursion.
