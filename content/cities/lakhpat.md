Lakhpat is a small fortified town in western Kutch, Gujarat, set near the mouth of Kori Creek. The settlement is enclosed by roughly 7 km of 18th-century fort walls of hard brown stone, with round towers; the walls were rebuilt and expanded around 1801 under Fateh Muhammad. It was once a busy port. The 1819 earthquake changed the creek and silted the harbour, and the walled town is now quiet, with only a few hundred residents inside the fort.

## Places to Explore

- **Lakhpat Fort walls and gateway** — a state-protected monument (ASI listing S-GJ-63). Walk the gateway, towers and sections of the irregular polygon wall for open views toward the creek flats.
- **Pir Ghaus Muhammad tomb** — shrine of a Sufi saint associated with Lakhpat, inside the fort area; local tradition treats the site with respect across communities.
- **Guru Nanak gurudwara** — a Sikh shrine in the town linked to Guru Nanak’s travels in this region.
- **Creek edge and salt flats** — wide, flat views outside the walls; access and walking conditions change with tide and season, so ask locally before going far out.
- **Nearby Kutch circuit** — Lakhpat is a long day trip from Bhuj (roughly 140 km). Narayan Sarovar and Koteshwar are often combined by travellers heading this far west, with realistic road time.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Inside the town:** very limited. Expect simple tea, snacks and basic Gujarati or Kutchi vegetarian food if a stall or household eatery is open.
- **Day-trip planning:** carry water and a packed meal. Bhuj and Nakhatrana have the reliable thali, fafda-jalebi and dhaba options before or after the drive.
- **What to expect:** roti or rotla, dal, kadhi and seasonal vegetables rather than a night-market scene.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Lakhpat itself:** almost no formal hotels. Do not plan on an overnight unless a local stay is confirmed in advance.
- **Bhuj:** the practical base, with the widest range of lodges and hotels for a Kutch trip.
- **Nakhatrana or other highway towns:** possible mid-route stops if you are driving between Bhuj and western Kutch.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,800–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day if you eat in Bhuj; less choice inside Lakhpat.
- **Local transport:** a hired cab for the Bhuj–Lakhpat return is the main cost, often ₹2,500–₹4,500 for the vehicle depending on hours and stops.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Drive from Bhuj toward western Kutch → Lakhpat fort gateway, wall walk and shrines → creek-edge views if conditions are safe → return to Bhuj for dinner.

**Day 2:** Use Bhuj as a base for another Kutch stop such as the crafts villages, or continue toward Narayan Sarovar and Koteshwar only if road time fits the day.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season. Summer heat and the white salt flats make midday walking harsh.
- The walled town has few services. Fuel, cash and water are better sorted in Bhuj or Nakhatrana.
- Dress modestly at the dargah and gurudwara, and follow local photography guidance.
- This is a border-adjacent creek area. Stick to open public paths and do not wander onto restricted stretches.
