## Places to Explore
- **Surat Castle** — historic fort beside the Tapi.
- **Dumas Beach** — popular coastal escape.
- **Dutch Cemetery** — atmospheric colonial-era heritage site.
- **Sarthana Nature Park** — family-friendly nature attraction.
- **Tapi Riverfront** — useful for an evening city walk.

## Food & Local Areas
Surat is known for **locho, surti ghari, khawsa and sev khamani**. Explore **Chowk Bazaar, Adajan and Ghod Dod Road** for restaurants and snacks; **Dumas Road** is popular for evening food stops.

## Where to Stay
**Athwa, Adajan, Vesu and Ring Road** are practical areas for hotels and restaurants.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Surat Castle → old city → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Sarthana → Dumas Beach → evening riverfront.
