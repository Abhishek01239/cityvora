Jaisalmer is Rajasthan's **Golden City**, close to the Thar Desert. Its most prominent landmark is **Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Qila)**, a living fort containing shops, hotels and historic havelis. citeturn0search1

## Places to Explore

- **Jaisalmer Fort** — the city's defining living fort.
- **Patwon Ki Haveli** — an elaborately carved historic haveli complex. citeturn0search1
- **Nathmal Ji Ki Haveli** — known for detailed sandstone carving. citeturn0search1
- **Gadisar Lake** — historic lake and city landmark.
- **Sam Sand Dunes** — desert area for camel safaris and camping; Rajasthan Tourism also lists Khuri as another safari area. citeturn0search1
- **Kuldhara** — historic abandoned village near Jaisalmer. citeturn0search1

## Food to Try

- **Kanchan Shree Ice Cream, Dhibba Para** — popular local dessert stop.
- **The Trio, Mandir Palace Road** — Rajasthani and Indian dishes near the fort.
- **Saffron, Fort Road** — rooftop dining with traditional Rajasthani options.
- **Main Bazaar / Fort lanes** — good for kachori, samosa, lassi and local sweets.

Try **dal baati churma, ker sangri, gatte ki sabzi, pyaaz kachori and makhaniya lassi**.

**Food map:** [Find Jaisalmer food spots](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jaisalmer+Rajasthan+local+food)

## Where to Stay

- **Inside/around Jaisalmer Fort** — atmospheric and close to heritage sights.
- **Dhibba Para / Fort Road** — convenient for restaurants and sightseeing.
- **Sam Road** — useful if your priority is desert camps and dune excursions.

RTDC's Moomal Hotel is on Sam Road near the Collector's Office and lists access to the fort, havelis, Gadisar Lake and Sam Sand Dunes. citeturn0search15

## Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500–₹7,000
- **Premium/desert camp:** ₹8,000+
- **Food:** ₹300–₹1,500/day
- **Local transport:** ₹150–₹600/day
- **Hotels:** roughly ₹800–₹8,000+ per night; desert camps and peak-season rates can be higher.

Desert safari and camp packages vary considerably, so compare the current package inclusions before booking.

## Quick Plan

**Day 1:** Jaisalmer Fort → Patwon Ki Haveli → Nathmal Ji Ki Haveli → Gadisar Lake → old-city dinner.

**Day 2:** Kuldhara → Sam Sand Dunes → sunset camel/cultural experience → desert camp.
