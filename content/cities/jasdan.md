Jasdan is a municipal town in Rajkot district in the Saurashtra region. It is best known for the nearby Hingolgadh Nature Education Sanctuary and fort, along with local temples and traditional crafts.

## Places to Explore

- **Hingolgadh Nature Education Sanctuary & Fort** — a dry-deciduous sanctuary and historic fort/palace area a short drive from town; known for nature education camps, chinkara, nilgai and birdlife.
- **Local temples** — including Batuk Hanumanji Temple, Gupteshwar Mahadev and Swaminarayan temple complexes in and around town.
- **Ghela Somnath Temple area** — a significant regional shrine a short distance east of Jasdan.
- **Town markets and craft pockets** — known for traditional work including enamel/minakari-style items and local handicrafts.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town restaurants and dhabas:** Gujarati and Kathiyawadi thalis, simple vegetarian meals.
- **Market and roadside stalls:** thepla, fafda, dhokla, chai and seasonal snacks.
- **Eateries near the bus stand and main roads:** everyday local food suitable for short stops.

Vendor names and menus change; check on arrival.

## Where to Stay

- **Jasdan town centre:** practical for exploring the town itself.
- **Near Hingolgadh / highway lodges:** useful if focusing on the sanctuary and fort.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,100–₹2,400
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,800–₹5,500
- **Comfort:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,300/day.
- **Local transport / sanctuary access:** roughly ₹150–₹600/day depending on hired vehicle use.

Confirm current sanctuary entry and transport costs locally.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive in Jasdan → town temples and market → evening local food walk.

**Day 2:** Full or half-day visit to Hingolgadh Nature Education Sanctuary and fort → return via Ghela Somnath if time allows → depart toward Rajkot or other Saurashtra destinations.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable period for outdoor visits.
- Carry water, sun protection and comfortable walking shoes for the sanctuary.
- Check current sanctuary visiting rules and any nature-camp schedules.
- Monsoon months can make trails softer and greener but also hotter and more humid.
