Dhandhuka is a quiet municipal town in Ahmedabad district, set in the Bhal region. It is historically noted as the birthplace of the great Jain scholar Hemachandra and offers a low-key look at rural Gujarati town life.

## Places to Explore

- **Sites linked to Hemachandra** — the town is remembered as the birthplace of the 12th-century Jain scholar; a temple was historically associated with the site.
- **Local temples and shrines** — including spots of regional devotion such as Ramapir and other community temples.
- **Town lakes and open areas** — small water bodies and outskirts used by locals for evening walks.
- **Nearby countryside of the Bhal region** — flat agricultural landscape typical of this part of Gujarat.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town restaurants and hotels:** straightforward Gujarati thalis and simple vegetarian meals.
- **Market and street stalls:** fafda, jalebi, thepla, chai and seasonal snacks.
- **Local eateries near the bus stand / main road:** everyday Gujarati and Kathiyawadi-style food.

Confirm current stall and restaurant options on the ground.

## Where to Stay

- **Town centre / near the bus stand:** most practical for short visits.
- **Simple lodges along the main road:** useful if travelling between Ahmedabad and Bhavnagar.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹900–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,200–₹4,500
- **Comfort:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹200–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹80–₹350/day using autos or shared transport.

Prices fluctuate with season and demand.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and settle → explore town centre and local temples → evening market and snack stop.

**Day 2:** Visit sites connected with local Jain heritage → relaxed walk around a nearby lake or open area → depart toward Ahmedabad, Dholka or Bhavnagar.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the more comfortable season.
- The Bhal region can feel hot and dry outside winter months.
- Check local temple or shrine timings in advance.
- Carry water and sun protection for daytime walks.
