## Places to Explore
- **BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir** — principal heritage and pilgrimage landmark.
- **Ghela River** — local landscape and peaceful surroundings.
- **Gadhada old town** — temples, markets and local life.
- **Salangpur** — useful nearby pilgrimage excursion.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi food, gathiya, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Temple Road, Bus Stand and the central market**.

## Where to Stay

Temple visit → Ghela River area → local lunch → Salangpur excursion.
