Mansa is a town in Gandhinagar district, north of Ahmedabad and close to the state capital. It is known for the Mahudi Jain Temple in the same taluka, local temples, lakes and quiet Gujarati small-town life.

## Places to Explore

- **Mahudi Jain Temple (Mahudi)** — major Jain tirth dedicated to Padmaprabh and Ghantakarna Mahavir; busy around Kali Chaudas.
- **Darbargarh and local heritage** — historic fort-related structures in Mansa.
- **Local temples** — Gayatri Shaktipeeth, Umiya Mataji, Dwarkadhish, Swaminarayan and other neighbourhood shrines.
- **Malan Lake / Malav Lake and Chandrasar Pond** — local water bodies for a short walk.
- **Mansa Vav and Vav Darvaja** — stepwell and gate points of local interest.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town centre and highway eateries:** Gujarati thali, dhokla, khandvi, thepla, farsan and chai.
- **Near Mahudi:** simple pure-veg meals and sweets (including traditional sukhadi associated with the temple).
- Street snacks in the bazaar area; prefer busy, clean stalls.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Mansa town:** Small hotels and lodges suitable for a night or two.
- **Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad side:** Wider choice of hotels if you want more facilities.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding intercity trains or flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** autos, buses or hired car; Mahudi is a short trip.

Hotel prices vary by date and festival periods.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive Mansa → local temples or Darbargarh → evening lake or town walk and Gujarati meal.

**Day 2:** Morning visit to Mahudi Jain Temple → continue to Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable season.
- At Mahudi, follow temple rules; sukhadi is a traditional offering.
- Combine easily with Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad day trips.
- Carry modest clothing for temple visits.
