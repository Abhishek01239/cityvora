Rajkot is a major city in Saurashtra with a strong Gujarati cultural identity, local markets, museums and a food scene built around regional favorites.

## Places to explore

### Kaba Gandhi No Delo

This heritage site is associated with Mahatma Gandhi's early life in Rajkot and provides historical context for the city.

### Watson Museum

The museum is a useful stop for visitors interested in regional history, archaeology and cultural objects from Saurashtra.

### Local markets

Central Rajkot is well suited to exploring traditional shopping streets, textiles, snacks and everyday Gujarati city life.

## What to eat

Try Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, khichdi-kadhi, dhokla and other regional snacks. Rajkot's food culture is especially easy to explore through breakfast and evening snack stops.

## Practical tips

For a first visit, combine heritage stops with market and food exploration rather than crossing the city repeatedly. Carry water and plan outdoor walking around the day's weather.
