Rajkot is a compact Saurashtra city where heritage sites, museums, Gujarati food, traditional markets and everyday local life can fit into a short trip.

## Places to Explore

- **Kaba Gandhi No Delo** — heritage house and Gandhi-related museum.
- **Watson Museum** — history and culture of Saurashtra in Jubilee Garden.
- **Rotary Dolls Museum** — family-friendly cultural collection.
- **Jubilee Garden** — central green space near cultural attractions.
- **Rashtriya Shala** — associated with khadi, handloom and traditional crafts.
- **Bangdi Bazaar** — traditional shopping for bangles, textiles and local merchandise.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Race Course Ring Road:** Restaurants, cafés and snack options across a busy modern part of the city.
- **Yagnik Road:** Central area with restaurants, sweets and Gujarati snacks.
- **Dhebar Road / old-city market:** Local shopping mixed with snack and farsan shops.
- **Kalawad Road:** Broad choice of restaurants and contemporary food.

Try **Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, dhokla, khandvi, farsan and khichdi-kadhi**. Individual stall names and hours can change, so verify locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Kalawad Road:** Modern hotels, restaurants and easy access to newer parts of Rajkot.
- **Yagnik Road:** Central for shopping and city sightseeing.
- **Race Course:** Convenient for restaurants and leisure areas.
- **Near Rajkot Junction:** Useful for short rail-based visits.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding travel to Rajkot:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹400/day using autos, buses and cabs.

Hotel prices vary by dates, location and season. Check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Kaba Gandhi No Delo → Watson Museum → Jubilee Garden → old-city market → Gujarati dinner.

**Day 2:** Rotary Dolls Museum → Rashtriya Shala → shopping around Bangdi Bazaar/Yagnik Road → evening food stop.

## Best Time to Visit

October to February is generally comfortable for walking and outdoor sightseeing. Summer can be much hotter, so plan outdoor activities earlier or later in the day.

## Practical Tips

- Check museum timings before visiting.
- Keep comfortable footwear for market and heritage walks.
- Compare products and craftsmanship before buying local handicrafts.
- Leave time for Gujarati snacks and local markets rather than only major attractions.
