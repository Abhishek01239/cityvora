Radhanpur is a town in Patan district and the seat of a former princely state ruled by the Babi family. It sits on the dry north-Gujarat plain, with old market lanes and a road that continues toward Santalpur and the northern fringe of the Rann of Kutch.

## Places to Explore

- **Old town and palace area** — Radhanpur was the capital of the Babi nawabs. Carved wooden houses from the palace have been documented; ask locally which fragments or lanes are still visible.
- **Historic gates and bazaar streets** — the compact old core is the main walk in town.
- **Banas river country** — the seasonal Banas flows through this part of north Gujarat; the riverbed is usually dry outside the monsoon.
- **Santalpur road** — the practical onward route if you are heading toward the edge of the Rann rather than staying in town.
- **Nearby Patan or Palanpur** — Rani ki Vav is in Patan city, not in Radhanpur; treat it as a separate day if that is the main goal.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town bazaar:** fafda, jalebi, ganthiya, tea and simple north-Gujarat snacks.
- **Local thali spots near the bus stand:** khichdi, kadhi, bajra or wheat rotla, and seasonal vegetables.
- **Highway stalls on the Patan–Radhanpur road:** useful for a quick meal if you are only passing through.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Radhanpur town:** a limited set of lodges; book ahead on festival or Rann-season weekends.
- **Patan or Palanpur:** more hotel choice if Radhanpur is a day visit.
- **Santalpur side:** basic stays only, better if your next stop is the Rann edge.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹900–₹1,800
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,000–₹3,800
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹4,500+ (usually means staying in Palanpur or Patan)
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹700/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹600/day in town; a hired car costs more for Santalpur or Patan.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive from Patan or Palanpur → old bazaar and gate walk → palace area → evening market snacks.

**Day 2:** Morning in town → drive toward Santalpur if you want the Rann fringe, or return to Patan for Rani ki Vav.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the sensible season; late summer is very hot and dry.
- Do not expect a maintained palace museum circuit — much of the interest is the old town itself.
- Cash is useful in the bazaar. ATMs are easier in Patan and Palanpur.
- If the Rann is the real destination, start early; distances look short on a map and take longer on rural roads.
