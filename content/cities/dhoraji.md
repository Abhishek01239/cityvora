## Places to Explore
- **Dhoraji Fort gates** — distinctive historic landmark.
- **Gondal** — easy nearby palace-and-heritage excursion.
- **Dhoraji old city** — local architecture and markets.
- **Bileshwar** — nearby religious and rural destination.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Kathiyawadi dishes, Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Station Road, Bus Stand and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Central Dhoraji** works for a short stay; Rajkot and Gondal offer more choices.
[Search Dhoraji hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Dhoraji%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Simple Plan
Fort gates → old city → lunch → Gondal excursion.
