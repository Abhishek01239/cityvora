Dahod sits in eastern Gujarat near the Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh borders. It is a gateway to the area's tribal culture, forests and hill landscapes.

## What to See
- **Ratanmahal Wildlife Sanctuary** — a protected forest landscape in Dahod district.
- **Mangadh Hill** — a scenic and culturally significant hill area.
- **Bavka Shivmandir** — a historic temple listed by the district administration.

## Food
Explore **Station Road, Tower Road and the central market** for Gujarati meals and local snacks. Try **dal-pakwan, fafda-jalebi, khichdi-kadhi and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** railway-station area
- **Mid-range:** central Dahod
- **Higher-end:** limited; check current availability before travel

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,400/day budget; ₹2,400–₹4,500/day mid-range.
