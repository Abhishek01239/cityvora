Savarkundla (also written Savar Kundla) is a municipality in Amreli district, formed from the historically linked settlements of Savar and Kundla. It is known regionally for its weighing-scale manufacturing industry and for a distinctive Diwali tradition called Ingoriya ni Ladai. The town sits within easy reach of other Saurashtra destinations including Gir.

## Places to Explore

- **Swaminarayan Temple and local temples** — everyday places of worship that give a sense of the town’s religious life.
- **Darbargarh area** — associated in local memory with older fort-like structures linked to regional history.
- **Town markets** — practical for a short walk and a look at everyday commerce, including shops connected to the weighing-scale trade.
- **Nearby Gir National Park** — the major nature draw of the wider region (Asiatic lions); plan as a separate excursion with proper safari booking.
- **Surrounding Amreli district countryside** — mango orchards (including Kesar) and rural landscapes in season.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Central market and roadside stalls:** fafda-jalebi, dhokla, thepla, ganthiya and simple Gujarati thalis.
- **Local restaurants:** look for seasonal undhiyu in winter and everyday kadhi, dal and vegetable preparations.
- **Sweet shops:** regional Gujarati sweets when available.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Savarkundla town:** modest hotels and lodges suited to short stays.
- **Amreli or larger nearby towns:** more options if you need a wider choice.
- **Sasan Gir / Gir periphery:** better if your main purpose is the national park (book well ahead in peak season).

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+ (often a stay nearer Gir or a larger town)
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹500/day inside town; safari and longer inter-town travel cost extra.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Town temples and market walk → local thali lunch → evening exploration of the central area.

**Day 2:** Day trip toward Gir (if booked) or a slower circuit of nearby Amreli-district spots → return to Savarkundla or continue to another Saurashtra town.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most pleasant period for walking and outdoor plans.
- If combining with Gir, book official safari permits early; the park has strict quotas and timings.
- The town itself is compact; most interest is cultural and practical rather than monumental sightseeing.
- Diwali brings the local Ingoriya tradition — interesting to observe with respect if you are present then.
