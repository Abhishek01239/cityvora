Saputara is Gujarat’s hill station, set in the Dang district on the Gujarat–Maharashtra border. Cooler air, the lake, tribal Dang culture and short forest walks make it an easy two-day break from Surat, Valsad or Nashik.

## Places to Explore

- **Saputara Lake** — the town centrepiece, with a promenade and boat rides when the lake is open.
- **Sunset Point** — a short drive or walk for evening views over the Sahyadri ridges.
- **Ropeway** — a cable-car ride between the lake area and a hill viewpoint; confirm running hours locally.
- **Gira Waterfalls** — a seasonal fall on the Ambika, often paired with a Saputara trip in and after the monsoon.
- **Artist Village / tribal museum area** — displays and craft stalls linked to Dang communities; check what is open on the day.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Lake-front stalls and small eateries:** snacks, tea, Gujarati thali and simple North Indian meals aimed at day visitors.
- **Main market near the bus stand:** basic restaurants and sweet shops; expect limited late-night choice.
- **Dang staples:** undhiyu and thali in season, plus local corn, bamboo-shoot dishes and forest honey when available.
- **Nashik or Waghai** are better bases if you want a wider restaurant range after the hill stay.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Lake road:** closest to boats, the promenade and evening walks.
- **Hillside hotels above the town:** quieter rooms and ridge views; a short auto ride to the lake.
- **Waghai or Ahwa:** lower-priced stays if Saputara rooms are full on weekends.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000–₹6,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹7,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,200/day depending on stalls vs restaurant thalis.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹800/day for autos and shared jeeps to viewpoints; a private cab from Surat is higher.

Hotel prices jump on weekends and holidays; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive via Waghai → Saputara Lake and boat ride → Artist Village area → Sunset Point.

**Day 2:** Morning ridge walk or ropeway → Gira Waterfalls if the season allows → return toward Surat, Valsad or Nashik.

## Before You Go

- October to February is the clearest walking weather; monsoon months are greener but roads can be slippery.
- Weekends fill fast with visitors from Surat and Maharashtra.
- Carry a light jacket; evenings are cooler than the Gujarat plains.
- Check ropeway and boat timings before travelling.
