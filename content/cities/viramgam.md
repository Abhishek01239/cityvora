Viramgam is a railway town in Ahmedabad district, best known for Munsar Lake, a stone-edged reservoir associated with the Chaulukya period and ringed by small shrines. It works as a half-day or overnight stop west of Ahmedabad.

## Places to Explore

- **Munsar Lake** — historic lake with carved stone steps and shrines; the main reason to stop. It is a protected monument, so follow site rules.
- **Mansar Mata temple** — temple by the lake, often visited with the walk around the water.
- **Old town market** — compact commercial centre near the station for a short walk.
- **Other town lakes** — Gangasar and nearby tanks if you want a longer local loop.
- **Ahmedabad** — about 70 km east, the usual base or connection city.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Station Road / market:** Gujarati snacks, tea, fafda-jalebi and simple thali meals.
- **Lake approach:** Small stalls; do not rely on them for a full meal.
- **Ahmedabad:** Much wider restaurant choice if Viramgam is only a day trip.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Viramgam:** Basic hotels and lodges near the station and market.
- **Ahmedabad:** Better hotel range, about 1.5–2 hours by road or train, and the easier base.
- **Sanand / Bavla corridor:** Occasional highway stays if you are driving between Saurashtra and Ahmedabad.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹900–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,200–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹600/day in town; more for a cab from Ahmedabad.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Train or road from Ahmedabad → Munsar Lake and shrines → market walk.

**Day 2:** Morning lake loop → return to Ahmedabad for the old city or Sabarmati Ashram.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable for walking the lake steps.
- The monument area can be bright and exposed; carry water.
- Do not climb unstable stonework around the shrines.
- Check train times if you are using Viramgam junction as the connection.
