Dharampur is a town in Valsad district, south Gujarat, and the former capital of Dharampur State. The capital was shifted here in the 18th century under Dharamdevji. Today it is a market town on the edge of the Western Ghats, used as a base for Wilson Hills and as a stop for the Lady Wilson Museum. Vapi and Valsad are the larger rail and highway hubs nearby.

## Places to Explore

- **Lady Wilson Museum** — opened on 31 January 1928 by Lady Wilson, wife of Bombay Presidency governor Leslie Wilson, and built up by Vijayadevji of Dharampur. The state museum holds crafts, textiles, coins, natural-history specimens and objects collected for the old state. It is commonly closed on Wednesday; confirm hours before you go.
- **Wilson Hills** — a hill viewpoint in the Sahyadri range roughly 25–40 km from town, named in connection with Leslie Wilson. The drive is the outing; roads can be narrow and foggy in monsoon.
- **Town market** — everyday south-Gujarat bazaar for produce, snacks and tribal-area trade.
- **Forest edges** — short walks only on marked or locally recommended paths. This is a reserved-forest belt.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town stalls and sweet shops:** fafda, jalebi, bhajiya, dhokla and tea. South Gujarat also has a strong taste for undhiyu in winter and for simple coastal-influenced snacks closer to Valsad.
- **Thali eateries:** Gujarati vegetarian plates. Ask for local vegetables in season.
- **Wilson Hills:** a few snack stalls on clear days; do not rely on a full meal at the viewpoint.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Dharampur town:** modest hotels and lodges for one night before a hill drive.
- **Vapi:** more business hotels and highway food, useful if you are arriving by train.
- **Valsad:** a larger town base with more choice if Dharampur rooms are full.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,800
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** town autos are inexpensive; a Wilson Hills return cab is the larger cost, often ₹1,200–₹2,500 for the vehicle depending on wait time.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive via Vapi or Valsad → Lady Wilson Museum → town market and an early dinner.

**Day 2:** Morning drive to Wilson Hills → viewpoint and return before afternoon fog or traffic → continue toward Valsad, Vapi or Saputara if the wider hill trip is the plan.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the clearest window. Monsoon makes the ghat road slippery and viewpoints cloudy.
- Museum tickets are low-cost for Indian visitors; Wednesday closure is the usual pattern, but recheck.
- The hero image is Wilson Hills in Valsad district, the hill station associated with Dharampur, not a photo of the museum building.
- Mobile signal drops on the hill road. Download the route before you leave town.
