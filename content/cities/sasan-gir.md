Sasan Gir is the main gateway village to Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary, the only remaining home of the Asiatic lion in the wild.

## Places to Explore

- **Gir National Park safari** — jeep or bus safaris from Sasan (Sinh Sadan) or Devalia; book permits only on the official site (girlion.gujarat.gov.in). Park typically closed mid-June to mid-October.
- **Sinh Sadan** — Forest Department reception and orientation point near the main gate.
- **Crocodile Breeding Centre** — small nearby facility for mugger crocodiles.
- **Kamleshwar Dam** — scenic spot inside or near the sanctuary area.
- Nearby day options include Somnath Temple (about 40–50 km) or Junagadh/Girnar.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- Resorts and small eateries in Sasan village serve Gujarati thalis, vegetarian meals and some non-vegetarian options.
- Local snacks such as fafda, jalebi, undhiyu and dabeli are available seasonally.
- Many lodges include meal plans; otherwise eat at village restaurants or resort dining rooms.

Specific stall names change; confirm current options locally.

## Where to Stay

- Sasan village itself for proximity to the main gate and early safari departures.
- Jungle lodges and resorts slightly outside the village along the approach roads or river.
- Mix of budget guesthouses, mid-range resorts and higher-end wildlife lodges.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights or long-distance travel and safari permits:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹4,000–₹8,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹10,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹400–₹1,500/day (higher if relying on resort meals).
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹800/day; safari vehicle costs are extra and fixed by the forest department.

Safari permits sell out in peak season; book well ahead. Nearest airports are Rajkot or Diu; rail options via Junagadh or Veraval.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and check in → afternoon safari (if permits available) or orientation at Sinh Sadan → village walk or crocodile centre.

**Day 2:** Early morning safari → rest or short local drive → optional evening activity or departure. (Combine with Somnath if time allows.)

## Before You Go

- Park is closed roughly mid-June to mid-October; confirm exact dates yearly.
- Book official safari permits in advance; morning slots are popular for sightings.
- Wear earth-tone clothing for safaris; carry water, hat and insect repellent.
- Sightings are never guaranteed.
