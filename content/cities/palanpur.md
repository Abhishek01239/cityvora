## Places to Explore
- **Balaram Palace Resort** — heritage stay and forest surroundings.
- **Kirana-style old city markets** — useful for local shopping and food.
- **Banaskantha countryside** — good base for North Gujarat excursions.
- **Ambaji** — major pilgrimage destination nearby.
- **Balaram-Ambaji Wildlife Sanctuary** — nature and short hikes.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, gathiya and North Gujarati snacks around **Delhigate, Abu Highway and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Abu Highway, central Palanpur and Deesa Road** are practical areas.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Palanpur markets → local heritage → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Ambaji → Balaram-Ambaji area.
