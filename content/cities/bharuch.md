## Places to Explore
- **Golden Bridge** — historic crossing over the Narmada.
- **Bhrigu Rishi Temple** — linked to Bharuch's long religious history.
- **Jami Masjid** — old-city heritage.
- **Narmada riverfront and ghats** — useful for evening walks.
- **Kabirvad** — riverside excursion across the Narmada.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, sev-khamani, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Station Road, Zadeshwar Road and the old city**.

## Where to Stay
**Zadeshwar Road, Bharuch station area and central Bharuch** are convenient.

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** old city → Golden Bridge → Narmada evening.  
**Day 2:** Kabirvad → local food → Zadeshwar.
