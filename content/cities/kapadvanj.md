Kapadvanj is a taluka town in Kheda district, historically a trading stop inland from Khambhat. Its reason to visit is the 12th-century Kundvav stepwell and the carved torana linked to the Solanki ruler Siddhraj Jaysinh, both sitting inside the old town rather than in a separate monument park.

## Places to Explore

- **Kundvav** — a rectangular stepped tank with niches and pavilion corners, smaller and more worn than the Surya Kund at Modhera but the same broad idea.
- **Kapadvanj torana** — a kirti torana of pillars and architrave on the east side of the tank platform, among the better-preserved of Gujarat’s medieval toranas after Vadnagar.
- **Vohrawad** — the old Bohra neighbourhood, with wooden house fronts typical of central Gujarat merchant streets.
- **Mohar river edge** — the watercourse that made the town a practical place for tanks and wells.
- **Balasinor** — a short road trip for the dinosaur fossil park if you have a second afternoon.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market near Kundvav:** fafda, jalebi, tea and everyday Gujarati thali restaurants used by locals.
- **Highway stops on the Nadiad–Kapadvanj road:** simple vegetarian meals if you are only passing through.
- **Nadiad:** a larger choice of restaurants if you sleep there instead of in Kapadvanj.
- Sweets and namkeen from the main bazaar are the usual edible souvenir; do not expect a night-food market on the Ahmedabad scale.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Kapadvanj town:** a few basic lodges near the market, enough for one night.
- **Nadiad:** more hotels and the rail junction, about an hour by road.
- **Ahmedabad:** a practical base if Kapadvanj is a half-day add-on to the city.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹800–₹1,800
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,000–₹4,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹800/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹500/day for autos and ST buses; a cab from Ahmedabad costs more.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Ahmedabad or Nadiad to Kapadvanj → Kundvav and torana → Vohrawad lanes → market meal.

**Day 2:** Morning revisit for better light on the carvings → Balasinor fossil park if time allows → return toward Nadiad or Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is better for walking the open tank and lanes.
- The monuments sit among houses; there is no large ticketed campus, so ask locally for the current entrance path.
- Wear shoes you can take off if a temple courtyard is part of the visit.
- Nearest major railheads are Nadiad and Ahmedabad; the nearest airport is Ahmedabad.
