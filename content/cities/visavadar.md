Visavadar is a municipality in Junagadh district and a practical gateway to Sasan Gir and Gir National Park, the last natural home of the Asiatic lion. The town itself is modest; most visitors use it as a base or transit point for wildlife, pilgrimage and nearby hills.

## Places to Explore

- **Gir National Park / Sasan Gir** — roughly 25–30 km away; Asiatic lions, other wildlife and guided safaris (book officially).
- **Satadhar** — holy place associated with Aapa Giga, a local saint.
- **Tulsishyam** — known for hot springs (garam pani no zaro) and temple complex, a short distance away.
- **Kankai (Gir)** — temple area deeper in the forest region (~32 km).
- **Town area** — local market and everyday Junagadh-district life; former Bava Wala fort site is of local historical note.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town eateries and stalls:** Gujarati / Kathiyawadi thali, rotla, sev-tameta, undhiyu (seasonal), farsan and chai.
- **On the way to Sasan Gir:** simple dhabas and local meals; confirm hygiene and hours.
- Vegetarian options dominate; ask for current specials.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Visavadar town:** Basic hotels and lodges for a quiet overnight.
- **Sasan Gir area (nearby):** Wider range of resorts, forest lodges and hotels catering to safari visitors — often preferred by wildlife travellers.

**Hotel booking:** [Book Visavadar / Gir area hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Visavadar%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel and official safari fees:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000–₹6,000
- **Comfort (especially near Gir):** ₹7,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** jeep/taxi to Gir or nearby sites adds cost; share or hire as needed.

Safari permits and vehicle charges are separate and must be booked through official channels. Hotel rates rise in peak wildlife season.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive Visavadar → local temple or Satadhar if time → overnight or transfer toward Sasan Gir.

**Day 2:** Morning safari (if booked) or Tulsishyam / Kankai visit → return via Visavadar or continue to Junagadh / Somnath circuit.

## Before You Go

- Book Gir safari permits well in advance through official Gujarat tourism / forest department channels.
- October–March is generally pleasant; check specific park closures and rules.
- Carry water, sun protection and modest clothing for temples.
- Network coverage can be patchy near forest areas.
