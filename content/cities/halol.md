Halol is a town in Panchmahal district and a practical gateway to the Champaner-Pavagadh heritage and pilgrimage landscape.

## Places to See

Pavagadh Hill, Kalika Mata Temple, Champaner archaeological zone and Jami Masjid.

## Food to Try

Gujarati and Kathiyawadi food around Halol market and the Pavagadh approach; try sev-usal, bhajiya, fafda-jalebi and thali.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=halol%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Halol town is convenient for road access; Pavagadh-side stays suit pilgrimage trips.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=halol%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹900–1,600/day; mid-range ₹1,800–3,200/day; food ₹250–600/day; local transport ₹150–400/day; hotels ₹800–3,500/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
