## Places to Explore
- **Parnera Hill and Fort** — hilltop views and local heritage.
- **Tithal Beach** — popular coastal outing nearby.
- **Wilson Hills** — cooler hill destination for a longer trip.
- **Udvada** — Parsi heritage and food destination nearby.
- **Dharampur** — useful base for forest and hill excursions.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati snacks, Parsi-influenced food in nearby Udvada, farsan and sweets around **Station Road, Tithal Road and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Station Road, Tithal Road and central Valsad** are practical areas.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Parnera Hill → Valsad food walk → Tithal.  
**Day 2:** Udvada or Dharampur day trip.
