Bavla is a small municipal town in Ahmedabad district, known locally for its Jain and Swaminarayan temples, a quiet lake area and everyday Gujarati street food. It works well as a short stop or day trip from Ahmedabad.

## Places to Explore

- **Bavla Jain Temple (Shri Shankheshwar Parshwanath Jain Derasar)** — a significant local Jain shrine with traditional architecture.
- **Bavla Lake** — a relaxed local spot for evening walks and birdwatching.
- **Swaminarayan Temple Bavla** — an active community temple for Swaminarayan devotees.
- **Local markets and stepwell remnants** — small heritage and daily-life pockets around the town centre.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town centre and highway-side stalls:** classic Gujarati snacks such as dhokla, thepla, fafda, dabeli and chai.
- **Lake-view and local restaurants:** simple thalis, Kathiyawadi-style meals and vegetarian Gujarati fare.
- **Street-food corners near the market:** panipuri, pav bhaji and regional snacks.

Stall names and timings change often, so confirm locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Town centre / near the railway station:** convenient for short stays and local exploration.
- **Highway-adjacent lodges:** practical if arriving by road from Ahmedabad or continuing further.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day depending on street food vs restaurant meals.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹400/day using autos or shared vehicles.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and settle → Bavla Jain Temple → town market and snack stop → evening at Bavla Lake.

**Day 2:** Swaminarayan Temple → local stepwell or heritage corners → relaxed thali lunch → depart or continue to Ahmedabad / Dholka.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable for walking.
- Start outdoor visits early in hotter months.
- Confirm temple timings on the day of visit.
- Comfortable footwear helps for market and lake walks.
