## Places to Explore
- **Tower area** — central historic city landmark.
- **Jami Masjid** — old architectural heritage.
- **Green Mosque** — important historic mosque.
- **Khambhat Gulf** — coastal setting and historic trading context.
- **Local agate craft** — Khambhat is known for agate and stone craft traditions.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati snacks, khichdi-kadhi, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Tower area, Jami Masjid Road and the central market**.
