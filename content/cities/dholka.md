## Places to Explore
- **Khan Masjid** — historic Islamic architecture.
- **Jami Masjid** — important old-city heritage.
- **Malav Talav** — historic water body.
- **Old Dholka streets** — useful for heritage walks.
- **Ahmedabad** — easy nearby extension.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Dholka market, Bus Stand Road and the old city**.

## Where to Stay
Most visitors use **Ahmedabad** for a wider choice of hotels; Dholka works for a short local visit.

## Simple Plan
Morning heritage walk → local lunch → Malav Talav → return toward Ahmedabad.
