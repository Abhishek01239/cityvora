Rajpipla is a former princely capital on the Narmada, in Narmada district. The town still has palace buildings and a riverside setting, and it is the usual overnight stop before the Statue of Unity at Ekta Nagar.

## Places to Explore

- **Vijay Palace and old palace quarter** — Indo-European palace buildings from the Rajpipla state era; view from outside unless a visit is open.
- **Narmada ghat** — evening river walk in town.
- **Rajpipla market** — practical bazaar for snacks and local shopping.
- **Ekta Nagar / Statue of Unity** — the main regional sight, about 25 km away; book viewing slots in season.
- **Sardar Sarovar area** — dam viewpoint and river roads, usually combined with Ekta Nagar.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Tower Road / main market:** Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, gathiya and tea.
- **Bus stand area:** Simple meals before early Statue of Unity departures.
- **Ekta Nagar food court:** Backup meals on a monument day; expect tourist pricing.
- **Bharuch or Vadodara:** Wider restaurant choice if you are only transiting.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Rajpipla town:** Small hotels near the market and bus stand.
- **Ekta Nagar:** Tent city and resort stays closer to the statue, pricier in peak season.
- **Bharuch:** More mid-range hotels if you want a larger town base.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹1,000/day including a cab share to Ekta Nagar.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking. Statue of Unity tickets are extra.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Palace quarter → market → Narmada ghat evening walk.

**Day 2:** Ekta Nagar and Statue of Unity → dam viewpoint → return to Rajpipla.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the better season for outdoor monument time.
- Book Statue of Unity slots ahead on weekends and holidays.
- Carry water for the viewing gallery queues.
- Palace interiors are not always open; treat them as exterior sights unless staff confirm entry.
