Udaipur is known for its lakes, palace architecture, compact old-city streets and views that make it particularly suited to slower-paced sightseeing.

## Places to explore

### City Palace

The City Palace complex is a central landmark overlooking Lake Pichola and is one of the city's major heritage experiences.

### Lake Pichola

A boat ride or lakeside walk provides a different perspective on Udaipur's palace-lined waterfront and surrounding hills.

### Jagdish Temple and the old city

The lanes around Jagdish Temple are useful for exploring traditional shops, cafés, small galleries and everyday old-city life.

## What to eat

Try Rajasthani staples such as dal baati churma, along with local snacks and vegetarian thalis. Rooftop restaurants are popular for their lake and palace views.

## Practical tips

Many central attractions are close enough to combine on foot, although the old-city lanes can be crowded. Reserve extra time around sunset for waterfront viewpoints.
