Champaner, in Panchmahal district, is the base for the Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2004. The ruined city at the foot of Pavagadh Hill was briefly a capital of the Gujarat Sultanate under Mahmud Begada, and the hill above remains a pilgrimage site for Kalika Mata.

## Places to Explore

- **Jama Masjid** — the outstanding monument of the site, with a large courtyard and carved entrance, built in the late 15th century after Champaner became the sultanate capital.
- **Champaner citadel and city ruins** — gates, walls, tombs and mosques spread through the archaeological park. Do not try to see every structure in one short stop.
- **Pavagadh Hill** — fort remains on the climb, with the Kalika Mata temple at the top. A ropeway operates on the hill; check whether it is running before you rely on it.
- **Sat Kaman and helical stepwell** — among the clearer surviving structures in the lower city if you have a second half-day.
- **Halol** — the nearby town for practical food, buses and a few more stay options. Vadodara is about 45 km away.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Stalls near the archaeological park and Pavagadh road:** tea, snacks and simple Gujarati meals for day visitors.
- **Halol town market:** a better range of thali, fafda, ganthiya and evening snacks than the monument area.
- **Vadodara:** use it for a fuller dinner if you are based in the city and day-tripping to Champaner.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Champaner / Pavagadh:** limited rooms aimed at pilgrims and park visitors.
- **Halol:** the nearest ordinary town stay.
- **Vadodara:** the comfortable base, with many hotels, if you do not mind the drive.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+ (typically a Vadodara hotel)
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹800/day for shared transport around Halol; a cab from Vadodara costs more. Park entry and the ropeway are extra.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Jama Masjid → lower-city walls, Sat Kaman and stepwell → evening in Halol.

**Day 2:** Pavagadh Hill and Kalika Mata → fort viewpoints on the way down → return to Vadodara or Godhra.

## Before You Go

- October to March is best for walking the park and climbing Pavagadh. Carry water in warmer months and start early.
- The site is spread out. Comfortable shoes matter more than a long checklist.
- Dress modestly for the Kalika Mata temple. Photography rules can differ between the mosque, fort and temple — follow local signs.
- Check Archaeological Survey of India timings and ropeway status before you go.
