Jaipur blends monumental Rajput-era architecture with busy bazaars, craft traditions and a modern café and hospitality scene.

## Places to explore

### Amber Fort

Amber Fort sits outside the historic center and is one of Jaipur's signature heritage attractions. Set aside enough time to explore its courtyards and viewpoints.

### City Palace

The City Palace complex offers a close look at Jaipur's royal history, architecture and decorative arts in the heart of the old city.

### Hawa Mahal

The Hawa Mahal is an instantly recognizable pink façade near the old city's market streets. Pair the visit with a walk through the surrounding bazaars.

## Markets and crafts

Jaipur is known for textiles, block printing, jewelry, blue pottery and other crafts. Compare shops and ask about materials and workmanship before purchasing.

## What to eat

Look for Rajasthani specialties such as dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi and ker sangri, along with Jaipur's sweets and snacks.

## Practical tips

Jaipur can be hot outside the cooler season. Plan outdoor sightseeing earlier in the day, keep water with you, and group attractions by area to reduce travel time.
