Dhrangadhra is a town in Surendranagar district and the former seat of the Jhala rulers of Dhrangadhra State. It sits on the rail line between Ahmedabad and Saurashtra and is a practical overnight stop before the Little Rann of Kutch.

## Places to Explore

- **Dhrangadhra town centre** — a compact market town with the old princely-state layout still readable in the streets around the Raj quarter.
- **Dhrangadhra railway station** — a useful junction on the Ahmedabad–Hapa route, and the easiest way in without a car.
- **Little Rann of Kutch / Wild Ass Sanctuary** — the open salt desert north of town; most safari lodges are toward Dasada and Zainabad, not inside Dhrangadhra itself.
- **Halvad** — a nearby town with its own lakeside and palace associations, easy to add by road.
- **Surendranagar** — the district headquarters, with more shops and bus connections.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Station road and main bazaar:** Gujarati thali, fafda, dhokla and tea stalls used by rail passengers.
- **Highway dhabas toward Halvad and Viramgam:** simple vegetarian meals if the town market has closed.
- **Kathiawadi plates:** bajra rotla, ringan bharta and khichdi-kadhi appear in local kitchens in season.
- **Dasada lodges:** a wider set-menu choice if you continue to the Rann for the night.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Near the railway station:** simplest lodges for a train connection.
- **Town centre:** short walks to the bazaar.
- **Dasada / Zainabad:** better if the Little Rann safari is the main reason for the trip.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹600/day in town; a private jeep toward the Rann costs more, and sanctuary fees are extra.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive by train → station-road meal → town bazaar and old Raj quarter → evening walk.

**Day 2:** Road toward Dasada or Halvad → Little Rann edge if permits and a vehicle are arranged → return toward Ahmedabad or Rajkot.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the right window for the Rann; summer is harsh and the desert is less visited.
- Wild ass safaris need a vehicle and usually a local lodge or guide; do not plan to walk into the sanctuary.
- Stone-quarry trucks use some approach roads; allow extra time.
- Ahmedabad is the nearest major airport.
