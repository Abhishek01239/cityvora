Becharaji (also spelled Bahucharaji) is a town and taluka headquarters in Mehsana district, north Gujarat. Travellers come for the Bahuchara Mata temple, a working Shakti shrine with a large courtyard rather than a museum complex. The goddess is widely worshipped in this part of Gujarat, and the temple is an important pilgrimage site for the hijra community as well as other devotees. The town itself is a market stop on the roads that link Mehsana, Chanasma and the Patan side of north Gujarat.

## Places to Explore

- **Bahuchara Mata temple** — the main shrine in town. Allow time for darshan, the courtyard and the shops selling prasad, flowers and local souvenirs. Festival days, especially around Navratri, are crowded.
- **Temple approaches and bazaar** — the lanes outside the complex are the practical town centre for snacks, puja items and short breaks between visits.
- **Becharaji market** — everyday north Gujarat bazaar for provisions if you are passing through by road.
- **Mehsana** — the district city, a better base for hotels and rail if Becharaji is a day stop.
- **Modhera and Patan** — possible add-ons by vehicle. The Sun Temple at Modhera and Rani ki Vav at Patan are separate published stops, not a walk from the temple.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Temple-side stalls:** tea, fafda, ganthia, simple sweets and snack plates aimed at pilgrims.
- **Town market:** everyday Gujarati vegetarian meals, kadhi, rotli and dal. Choice thins after dark.
- **Mehsana:** a wider restaurant range if you stay in the district city instead of Becharaji.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Becharaji:** a small set of lodges and dharamshala-style stays near the temple. Enough for one night if booked, thin on choice.
- **Mehsana:** more hotels, the rail station and easier onward buses.
- **Ahmedabad or Patan:** practical city bases if Becharaji is only a daytime temple stop on a north Gujarat loop.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,400–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+ if you stay in Mehsana or Ahmedabad and day-trip
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹800/day.
- **Local transport:** buses and shared vehicles from Mehsana are inexpensive. A hired cab for a temple stop plus return is often ₹1,500–₹3,500 depending on the start city.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Road or bus from Mehsana or Ahmedabad → Bahuchara Mata temple → town market → overnight in Becharaji or Mehsana.

**Day 2:** Morning return to the temple if you missed a quieter slot, or continue to Modhera and Patan by vehicle. Do not stack both heritage sites and a late Ahmedabad arrival unless the car is already arranged.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season for courtyard walking. Navratri and other shrine festivals are crowded; footwear and bags follow temple rules.
- Photography inside the sanctum is often restricted. Ask at the gate.
- The town is a pilgrimage stop, not a resort. Book a room before arriving on festival weekends.
- Road traffic on the Mehsana approaches is fast. Use official bus stops and crossings.
