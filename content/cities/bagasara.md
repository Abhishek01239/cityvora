Bagasara is a municipality in Amreli district on the northern bank of the Satladi river. Historically linked to the Vala Kathis, it remains a small Saurashtra town known for local markets, temples and traditional crafts such as imitation jewellery and embroidery.

## Places to Explore

- **Town bazaar and central lanes** — everyday Saurashtra market life, shops and local atmosphere.
- **Local temples** — including Swaminarayan Temple (Kukavav Road), Bageshwar Mahadev Mandir and other neighbourhood shrines.
- **Moti Haveli (Madanmohanji ni Moti Haveli)** — Pushtimarg-linked historic haveli.
- **Munjiyasar Dam area** — nearby water body and associated temples (Mantreshvar Mahadev, Khodiyar Mataji).
- **Atalji Garden and riverside stretches** — quiet local open spaces.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Central bazaar / town eateries:** Gujarati thali, thepla, khakhra, farsan and everyday vegetarian meals.
- **Street stalls:** chai, snacks and seasonal items; stall locations shift, so ask locally.
- Typical Saurashtra-Gujarati flavours dominate; non-vegetarian options are limited in many small-town spots.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Town centre:** Convenient for the bazaar and temples.
- **Near main roads / bus stand area:** Practical for onward travel within Amreli district.

Options are mostly small hotels and guesthouses rather than large chains. Confirm current availability on booking platforms.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding intercity travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,500
- **Comfort:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,000/day depending on thali vs simpler meals.
- **Local transport:** autos and shared vehicles, typically ₹100–₹400/day within town and nearby.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and explore the central bazaar → visit one or two local temples → evening walk and Gujarati meal.

**Day 2:** Munjiyasar Dam and nearby shrines → riverside or garden stop → depart or continue toward Amreli / Junagadh circuit.

## Before You Go

- October to March is more comfortable for walking.
- Summers are hot; start outdoor visits early.
- Carry cash for smaller shops and stalls.
- Confirm temple opening times locally.
