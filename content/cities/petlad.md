Petlad is a Charotar market town in Anand district, known for brass and copper utensils, tobacco trade and a tight cluster of temples and bazaars. It sits between Anand and Borsad and is easy to cover in a day from Vadodara or Anand.

## Places to Explore

- **Petlad brass and copper market** — the town's best-known shopping stop for utensils and local metalwork.
- **Swaminarayan Mandir** — main temple complex in town, useful as a calm mid-day stop.
- **Chamunda Mata Temple** — local pilgrimage spot on the edge of town.
- **Station Road bazaar** — everyday market for snacks, cloth and household goods.
- **Anand and Borsad** — nearby Charotar towns for dairy stops and wider food options.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Station Road:** Fafda, jalebi, gathiya, tea and simple Gujarati thalis.
- **Tower / main bazaar:** Evening snack stalls and sweet shops.
- **Bus stand area:** Practical meals before onward buses to Anand or Borsad.
- **Anand:** Larger restaurant choice if you base yourself there.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Petlad town:** Small hotels and lodges near the station and bus stand.
- **Anand:** Better hotel range, about 20 km away, and a practical overnight base.
- **Vadodara:** Wider stay choice if Petlad is one stop on a Charotar circuit.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹800–₹1,800
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,000–₹4,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹4,800+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹80–₹400/day using autos, town buses and shared cabs.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Station Road bazaar → brass market → Swaminarayan Mandir → evening snacks.

**Day 2:** Chamunda Mata Temple → Borsad or Anand food stop → return.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable time for market walks.
- Brass shops can close for afternoon rest; go in the morning.
- Bargain politely in the utensil market and check weight before buying.
- Keep small cash for autos and snack stalls.
