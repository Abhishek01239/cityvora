Mansa is a town in Gandhinagar district, north of Ahmedabad and close to the state capital. It is best known as the taluka that includes the important Mahudi Jain Temple and offers a quiet Gujarati small-town base with temples, lakes and easy road access.

## Places to Explore

- **Mahudi Jain Temple (Mahudi)** — major Svetambara Jain tirth dedicated to Padmaprabh and Ghantakarna Mahavir; popular especially around Kali Chaudas.
- **Darbargarh / local heritage structures** — historic fort-related site in Mansa.
- **Local temples** — Gayatri Shaktipeeth, Umiya Mataji, Dwarkadhish, Swaminarayan and other neighbourhood shrines.
- **Malan Lake / Malav Lake and Chandrasar Pond** — local water bodies for a short stroll.
- **Mansa Vav and Vav Darvaja** — stepwell and gate points of local interest.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town centre and highway eateries:** Gujarati thali, dhokla, khandvi, thepla, farsan and chai.
- **Near Mahudi:** simple pure-veg meals and sweets (including the traditional sukhadi offering associated with the temple).
- Street snacks are available in the bazaar area; quality varies — choose busy, clean stalls.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Mansa town:** Small hotels and lodges suitable for a night or two.
- **Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad side:** Wider choice of hotels if you prefer more facilities and are touring the capital region.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding intercity trains/flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** autos, buses or hired car; day trips to Mahudi are short and inexpensive.

Hotel prices vary by date and festival periods (especially around Jain observances at Mahudi).

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive Mansa → local temples or Darbargarh → evening at a lake or town walk and Gujarati meal.

**Day 2:** Morning visit to Mahudi Jain Temple → return via Mansa or continue to Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable season.
- At Mahudi, follow temple rules; sukhadi is a traditional prasad/offering.
- Combine easily with Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad day trips.
- Carry modest clothing for temple visits.
