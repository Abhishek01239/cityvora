Mahuva is a coastal town in Bhavnagar district known for agriculture, its sea-facing setting and access to nearby beaches and temples.

## Places to See

Mahuva coast, nearby Nishkalank Mahadev Temple, local markets and surrounding coconut-growing countryside.

## Food to Try

Seafood and Gujarati food around the main market and coastal road; try Kathiyawadi thali, bhajiya and seasonal local produce.

## Where to Stay

Main market and coastal approach roads are convenient for short stays.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=mahuva%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,400/day; mid-range ₹1,600–2,800/day; food ₹250–600/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
