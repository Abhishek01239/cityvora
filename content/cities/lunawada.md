Lunawada is a historic town in Mahisagar district with connections to the former princely state and nearby heritage sites.

## What to See
- **Kaleshwari** — a historic site near Lunawada with ancient remains, stepwells and temples.
- **Mahisagar countryside** — good for short scenic drives.
- **Local heritage areas** — remnants of the former princely-state landscape.

## Food
Explore the **main market and bus-stand area** for Gujarati snacks and simple local meals. Try **fafda-jalebi, khaman, sev-tameta and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** central market
- **Mid-range:** main-road hotels
- **Higher-end:** consider Godhra or Vadodara for greater choice

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,300/day budget; ₹2,300–₹4,500/day mid-range.
