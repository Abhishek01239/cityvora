Ankleshwar is a major industrial city in Bharuch district, known for its business activity, temples and easy access to Bharuch and the Narmada region.

## Places to See

Valia Palace area, GIDC industrial zone, Shri Ankleshwar Mahadev Temple and nearby Bharuch heritage sites.

## Food to Try

Local Gujarati thali and farsan around Station Road and GIDC; try khichdi-kadhi, fafda-jalebi and sev-khamani.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ankleshwar%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Station Road and Bharuch-Ankleshwar corridor offer practical budget and business hotels.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ankleshwar%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹900–1,500/day; mid-range ₹1,800–3,000/day; food ₹250–600/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹800–3,500/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
