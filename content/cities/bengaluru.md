Bengaluru mixes large green spaces and historic neighborhoods with technology, design, cafés, breweries and a busy contemporary cultural scene.

## Places to explore

### Cubbon Park

Cubbon Park is a central green space that works well for a relaxed morning walk and a break from the city's traffic.

### Bengaluru Palace

The palace offers a glimpse into the city's royal-era history and distinctive architecture.

### Vidhana Soudha

Vidhana Soudha is one of Bengaluru's best-known civic landmarks and can be combined with a wider central-city itinerary.

## Neighborhoods

Indiranagar and Koramangala are popular for dining and nightlife. Malleswaram and Basavanagudi offer a more traditional neighborhood experience with markets, temples and long-running eateries.

## What to eat

Start with Bengaluru staples such as masala dosa, idli-vada and filter coffee. The city also has a broad modern restaurant scene spanning Indian and international cuisines.

## Practical tips

Traffic can significantly affect journey times. Build buffers into your itinerary and cluster nearby activities rather than trying to cross the city repeatedly in one day.
