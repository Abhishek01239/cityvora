Bengaluru mixes green spaces and historic neighborhoods with technology, cafés, shopping and a broad modern food scene.

## Places to Explore

- **Cubbon Park** — relaxed morning walk in central Bengaluru.
- **Bengaluru Palace** — royal-era architecture and history.
- **Vidhana Soudha** — iconic civic landmark.
- **Lalbagh Botanical Garden** — large landscaped garden and plant collection.
- **Malleswaram & Basavanagudi** — traditional neighborhoods, temples and old eateries.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **VV Puram Food Street:** Popular evening street-food stretch; try dosa, idli, vada, chats and sweets.
- **Malleswaram 8th Cross:** Long-running South Indian eateries and snack shops.
- **Indiranagar 12th Main:** Cafés, restaurants and contemporary food.
- **Koramangala:** Broad choice of casual dining and international cuisines.

Vendor names and operating hours can change, so verify individual stalls locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Majestic:** Budget-friendly and excellent transport access.
- **Indiranagar:** Cafés, restaurants and nightlife nearby.
- **Koramangala:** Good for dining and a younger urban atmosphere.
- **MG Road / CBD:** Central for business and sightseeing.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500–₹7,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹8,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,500/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹600/day depending on Metro, bus, auto or cab use.

Hotel prices vary by dates, neighborhood and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Cubbon Park → Vidhana Soudha → central Bengaluru → VV Puram food walk.

**Day 2:** Bengaluru Palace → Malleswaram/Basavanagudi → Lalbagh → Indiranagar dinner.

## Practical Tips

Traffic can be heavy. Cluster nearby sights together and use Metro where practical. Check attraction timings before leaving.
