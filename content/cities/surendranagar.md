## Places to Explore
- **Dholi Dhaja Dam** — popular local outing.
- **Wadhwan heritage area** — historic architecture near Surendranagar.
- **Chotila** — temple and hill destination for a day trip.
- **Little Rann of Kutch** — wildlife and landscape excursion.
- **Local textile and craft markets** — useful for regional shopping.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Station Road, Wadhwan and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Surendranagar city centre and Wadhwan** offer convenient bases.

- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**

- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Wadhwan heritage → markets → food.  
**Day 2:** Chotila or Little Rann excursion.
