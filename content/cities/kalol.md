Kalol is a north Gujarat town in Gandhinagar district with strong industrial and commercial links to Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar.

## Places to See

Kalol town centre, local temples, nearby Adalaj and Gandhinagar attractions, and the wider north Gujarat circuit.

## Food to Try

Gujarati snacks and Kathiyawadi meals around Station Road and Main Bazaar; try khaman, fafda-jalebi, sev-khamani and thali.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=kalol%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Station Road and the highway corridor offer the most practical stays.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=kalol%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,400/day; mid-range ₹1,600–2,800/day; food ₹200–550/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
