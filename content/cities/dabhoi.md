Dabhoi is an old fortified town in Vadodara district, historically linked with Darbhavati. The surviving stone gates, especially Hira Gate, are the reason most visitors stop here on a day trip from Vadodara.

## Places to Explore

- **Hira Gate (Hira Bhagol)** — elaborately carved eastern gate of the old fort, with sculpture and a Kalika Mata temple beside it.
- **Other fort gates** — Nagar, Mahudi and Vadodari gates still mark the old town walls; walk between them in the morning.
- **Kalika Mata Temple** — temple next to Hira Gate, often visited with the gate itself.
- **Old town lanes** — compact market streets inside the former walls.
- **Vadodara** — about 30 km away, for a longer heritage day if Dabhoi is a half-day stop.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market:** Gujarati snacks, fafda, gathiya and simple thali meals.
- **Bus stand / station side:** Practical tea and breakfast stops before a gate walk.
- **Vadodara:** Wider restaurant choice if you are based in the city and only day-tripping to Dabhoi.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Dabhoi:** Basic lodges near the market and bus stand for an overnight stop.
- **Vadodara:** Much wider hotel choice, about 45–60 minutes by road, and the usual base for this trip.
- **Ahmedabad:** Possible but long as a day trip; better if you are already moving between Ahmedabad and Vadodara.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹800–₹1,800
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,000–₹4,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹4,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day depending on street food vs restaurants.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹700/day using buses, autos and a cab from Vadodara.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Vadodara arrival → afternoon Hira Gate and Kalika Mata → old-town snack walk.

**Day 2:** Remaining fort gates in the morning → return to Vadodara for Laxmi Vilas Palace or the riverfront.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable for walking the gates.
- Start early in hotter months and carry water; there is limited shade on the walls.
- Check temple timings before travelling.
- Wear comfortable shoes for uneven stone paths.
