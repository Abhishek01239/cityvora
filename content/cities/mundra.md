Mundra is a coastal town in Kutch on the Gulf of Kutch, known today for one of India’s largest private ports and for an older settlement of temples, markets and salt flats. It works as a stop between Bhuj and Mandvi.

## Places to Explore

- **Mundra Port area** — the working harbour on the Gulf of Kutch; public access to operational docks is restricted, so treat it as a drive-by landscape rather than a walk-in site.
- **Old Mundra bazaar** — the compact market town with Kutchi traders, cloth shops and everyday food stalls.
- **Shri Navdurga Temple** — a local temple stop in town; confirm darshan timings on arrival.
- **Coast and salt pans** — flat Gulf shoreline west of town, best in clear winter light.
- **Mandvi and Bhuj** — easy add-ons for the beach palace at Mandvi and the museum circuit in Bhuj.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town bazaar:** Kutchi dabeli, fafda-jalebi in the morning, kadhi-khichdi and simple thalis.
- **Highway dhabas on the Bhuj–Mandvi road:** reliable vegetarian meals if the old town is closed for the afternoon.
- **Coastal stretches:** fish dishes appear in some eateries; ask what was landed that day rather than assuming a fixed menu.
- **Bhuj:** wider restaurant choice if you overnight there instead of Mundra.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Mundra town:** basic lodges near the market and bus stand, useful for an early port-road start.
- **Mandvi:** better beach and palace access, about an hour by road.
- **Bhuj:** more hotels and restaurants, with Mundra as a day trip.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,000/day on thalis and snacks.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹900/day for autos and shared jeeps; a cab to Mandvi or Bhuj costs more.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Bhuj to Mundra → old bazaar and Navdurga Temple → coastal or salt-pan drive → overnight in Mundra or Mandvi.

**Day 2:** Mandvi shipbuilding yards and beach if continuing south → return toward Bhuj.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season; summer on the Kutch coast is hot and dry.
- Port zones are industrial. Do not expect a public promenade inside the terminal.
- Carry cash for small bazaar stalls.
- Bhuj airport is the usual air gateway.
