## Places to Explore
- **Amul Dairy area** — Anand is closely associated with India's dairy cooperative movement.
- **Sardar Patel Memorial, Karamsad** — nearby heritage destination.
- **Anand city markets** — local food and shopping.
- **Vallabh Vidyanagar** — educational township with a relaxed atmosphere.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi, chaas and local dairy products around **Vallabh Vidyanagar, Anand city centre and Amul Dairy Road**.

## Where to Stay
**Anand city centre and Vallabh Vidyanagar** are practical areas.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Anand city → dairy heritage → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Karamsad → Vallabh Vidyanagar → local markets.
