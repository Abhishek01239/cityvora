Kheda (formerly Kaira) is a town in Kheda district at the confluence of the Vatrak and Shedhi rivers. It was once the administrative headquarters of the district and a British military station until the cantonment moved to Deesa around 1830. On 22 March 1918 Mahatma Gandhi launched the Kheda Satyagraha here against British tax demands during famine.

## Places to Explore

- **Old town and river confluence** — the meeting point of the Vatrak and Shedhi rivers and the compact historic core.
- **Meldi Mata Temple** — a local temple known for an annual fair, traditionally held in February.
- **Khedia Hanuman Temple** — a Hanuman shrine on Hanuman Tekro, associated with local tradition.
- **British-era remains** — traces of the former cantonment and older civic buildings; ask locally what is still visible.
- **Nearby Dakor and Nadiad** — Ranchhodrai Temple at Dakor and Santram Temple at Nadiad are short drives and major regional draws.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town bazaar:** fafda, jalebi, ganthiya, tea and everyday Gujarati snacks.
- **Local thali spots:** khichdi, kadhi, rotla or roti with seasonal vegetables, typical of the Charotar region.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Kheda town:** modest hotels and lodges suited to short stays.
- **Nadiad or Anand:** larger nearby centres with more hotel variety and easier rail connections.
- **Vadodara or Ahmedabad:** wider choice if combining with larger cities.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,300
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,600–₹5,200
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹280–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹80–₹450/day; buses and autos are common.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Walk the old town and river confluence → Meldi Mata or Hanuman temple → local market and food stop.

**Day 2:** Morning visit to Dakor Ranchhodrai Temple or Nadiad → return via Kheda or continue toward Vadodara / Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable.
- The Kheda Satyagraha is a historical marker rather than a large visitor complex; the town itself is practical.
- Check temple timings and festival dates (especially the Meldi Mata fair).
- Keep comfortable footwear for riverbank and town walks.
