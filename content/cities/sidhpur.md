Sidhpur is a historic sacred town on the Saraswati River, known for Bohra heritage homes, temples and the remains of Rudra Mahalaya.

## Places to See

Bohra Vad, Rudra Mahalaya ruins, Bindu Sarovar and Matru Gaya pilgrimage sites.

## Food to Try

Gujarati snacks and vegetarian meals around the old town and main market; try khaman, fafda-jalebi and Gujarati thali.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=sidhpur%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Near the main market and highway approaches for easy access to Patan and Modhera.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=sidhpur%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,400/day; mid-range ₹1,600–2,800/day; food ₹200–500/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
