## Places to Explore
- **Laxmi Vilas Palace** — the city's signature royal landmark.
- **Baroda Museum & Picture Gallery** — art, archaeology and natural-history collections.
- **Sayaji Garden** — large central garden with museums and family attractions.
- **Kirti Mandir** — historic memorial and architectural site.
- **EME Temple** — distinctive modern temple architecture.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, sev usal, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Alkapuri, Sayajigunj and Raopura**. **Mandvi** is useful for traditional snacks and street-food browsing.

## Where to Stay
Choose **Alkapuri, Sayajigunj or Fatehgunj** for easy access to transport, restaurants and attractions.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Laxmi Vilas Palace → Sayaji Garden → Museum.  
**Day 2:** Kirti Mandir → old-city food walk → Alkapuri.
