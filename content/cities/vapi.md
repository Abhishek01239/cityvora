Vapi is a major industrial and commercial city in south Gujarat, close to the Maharashtra border. It is useful as a business base and a gateway to Daman, Silvassa and the southern Gujarat coast.

## What to See
- **Daman** — an easy nearby coastal escape with forts, beaches and Portuguese-era heritage.
- **Silvassa** — nearby for gardens, tribal culture and nature.
- **Vapi GIDC** — the city's defining industrial district.

## Food
Look around **Char Rasta, Gunjan and Koparli Road** for Gujarati thali, farsan, South Indian food and quick street meals. Try **fafda-jalebi, khaman, locho and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** Char Rasta / railway-station area
- **Mid-range:** GIDC and main-city business areas
- **Luxury:** hotels along the Vapi–Daman corridor

**Typical budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500/day for a budget traveller; ₹2,500–₹5,000/day mid-range. Hotel prices vary by date.
