Khedbrahma is a temple town in Sabarkantha district, north Gujarat, on the banks of the Harnav river near the Aravalli foothills. The river splits the town into north and south sides. It has been a pilgrimage stop for centuries and later came under Idar State after earlier rule linked to the Parmaras, Chaulukyas and Pariharas. The town is a quieter base than Ambaji, with rare Brahma worship and an easy road link toward Polo Forest.

## Places to Explore

- **Brahma temple** — one of the uncommon Brahma temples in India. The standing shrine is generally dated to the 11th century; the four-faced image inside is older still. Wikivoyage places it in the old temple cluster of the town.
- **Ambika Mataji (Nana Ambaji)** — early medieval temple on the north-east side of town, rebuilt several times. This is the site in the guide photo.
- **Brahma Vav** — the old stepwell associated with the temple group.
- **Pankhnath Mahadev, Bhrigunath and Kshirjamba Mahalaxmi** — further shrines in and just outside town. Kshirjamba sits on a hillock with a view toward the Harnav.
- **Polo Forest (Vijaynagar)** — dry deciduous forest and 15th-century Hindu and Jain temple ruins near Abhapur, east of Khedbrahma along the Harnav. The Sharneshwar Shiva temple is the main ruin. It is a half-day drive, not a walk from the bus stand.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Sardar Chowk market:** daily fruit, vegetable and grocery stalls, plus simple Gujarati snacks and thali restaurants around the chowk.
- **Temple approaches:** tea, fafda and pilgrim meals. Do not expect a large night market.
- **Polo Forest day:** carry water and a packed meal. Food stalls near the forest gate are seasonal and limited.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Khedbrahma:** small lodges and pilgrim stays near the temples. Book ahead on weekends and Ambaji yatra days, when through-traffic rises.
- **Idar or Himatnagar:** larger hotel choice if you want a more comfortable base and a day trip up.
- **Polo Forest:** limited forest-side stays. Confirm before planning an overnight there.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,800
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day in town.
- **Local transport:** town autos are inexpensive. A hired cab for Khedbrahma and Polo Forest is often ₹1,800–₹3,000 for the vehicle for a day, depending on start point.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Brahma temple and Brahma Vav → Ambika Mataji → Harnav riverbank and Sardar Chowk → evening at Kshirjamba hillock if the light is good.

**Day 2:** Early drive to Polo Forest and the Sharneshwar ruins → return via Himatnagar or continue toward Ambaji only if road time allows.

## Before You Go

- October to March is best for the forest and temple walking. Monsoon roads toward Polo can be slow.
- Temple timings and footwear rules are local. Dress modestly and ask before photographing sanctums.
- Polo Forest ruins are state-archaeology sites. Do not climb unstable stonework.
- Mobile signal and fuel are more reliable in Khedbrahma or Himatnagar than inside the forest.
