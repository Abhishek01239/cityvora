Umreth is a municipal town and taluka headquarters in Anand district, in the Charotar belt of central Gujarat. It is often called the silk town of Charotar for its weaving of silk sarees, and the older streets still show the pol lanes typical of this region. The town sits between Nadiad and Dakor on the state highway, with a broad-gauge rail link toward Godhra that also serves the Dakor side. Elevation is low, around 44 metres, and the 2011 census counted about 33,800 residents in the municipality.

## Places to Explore

- **Old town and pols** — walk the older residential lanes for carved wooden fronts and the compact Charotar street pattern. There is no single ticketed monument; the interest is the fabric of the town.
- **Silk and handloom shops** — Umreth is known for silk sarees. Ask in the main market rather than assuming a fixed craft centre.
- **Town market and APMC side** — the working commercial core for cloth, grain and everyday shopping.
- **Dakor** — the Ranchhodrai Krishna temple at Dakor is the main pilgrimage stop nearby, a short road trip east. Festival days are extremely crowded.
- **Anand and Nadiad** — larger Charotar towns for the Amul dairy circuit, bigger markets and rail connections if Umreth is only a half-day stop.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market lanes:** everyday Gujarati snacks, fafda, ganthia, tea and simple thali meals. Winter menus in Charotar often include undhiyu and seasonal vegetables.
- **Highway and bus-stand side:** practical stops for travellers changing buses toward Anand, Nadiad or Dakor.
- **Dakor:** more pilgrim eateries around the temple town if you combine the trip; expect vegetarian food and festival queues.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Umreth:** limited formal hotels. Fine for a short stop if a lodge is confirmed, not a base with many options.
- **Anand:** the most convenient overnight base, with better hotel choice and rail links.
- **Nadiad or Dakor:** useful if the plan is built around the Krishna temple or the Ahmedabad–Vadodara highway.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day on local thali and snacks.
- **Local transport:** shared autos and GSRTC buses are cheap; a hired cab for Umreth plus Dakor is often ₹1,200–₹2,200 for the vehicle depending on hours.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive via Anand or Nadiad → Umreth market and old pols → silk-shop walk → overnight in Anand or Umreth if a room is confirmed.

**Day 2:** Morning at Dakor temple before peak crowds → return through Charotar towns, or continue to Vadodara or Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable walking season. Summer afternoons in Charotar are hot.
- Dakor is packed on full-moon days and major Krishna festivals. Go early and use official queues.
- Dress modestly at the temple and follow photography rules inside shrine areas.
- Umreth is a working town, not a resort. Carry cash for small shops.
