Chhota Udepur is known for tribal art, architecture and a distinct cultural landscape in eastern Gujarat.

## What to See
- **Chhota Udepur Museum** — presents tribal life, art, paintings and artefacts.
- **Kali Niketan** — a historic palace associated with the former princely state.
- **Hafeshwar** — a religious and natural attraction in the wider district.

## Food
Try Gujarati and tribal-region influenced meals around the **main market and bus stand**. Look for **rotla, local vegetables, khichdi-kadhi and Gujarati snacks**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** central market/bus stand
- **Mid-range:** main road
- **Higher-end:** Vadodara is the practical alternative for a wider hotel selection

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200/day budget; ₹2,200–₹4,500/day mid-range.
