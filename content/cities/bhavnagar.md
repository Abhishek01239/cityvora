## Places to Explore
- **Takhteshwar Temple** — hilltop views over the city.
- **Nilambag Palace** — heritage architecture.
- **Gaurishankar Lake** — peaceful evening stop.
- **Gandhi Smriti** — local historical and cultural attraction.
- **Palitana** — major temple destination for a day trip.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khichdi-kadhi, fafda-jalebi and Kathiyawadi dishes around **Waghawadi Road, Nilambaug and Ghogha Circle**.

## Where to Stay
**Waghawadi Road, Kalubha Road and Nilambaug** are convenient areas.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Takhteshwar → Nilambag → old-city food.  
**Day 2:** Palitana day trip or Velavadar Blackbuck National Park.
