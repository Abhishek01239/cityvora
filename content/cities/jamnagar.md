## Places to Explore
- **Lakhota Lake & Lakhota Palace** — the city's central landmark.
- **Pratap Vilas Palace** — heritage architecture.
- **Bala Hanuman Temple** — well-known city temple.
- **Khijadiya Bird Sanctuary** — excellent for birdwatching outside the city.
- **Jamnagar markets** — useful for local textiles, snacks and handicrafts.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi, gathiya and Kathiyawadi dishes around **Teen Batti, P.N. Marg and the old city**.

## Where to Stay
**P.N. Marg, Summair Club Road and central Jamnagar** are convenient.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Lakhota Lake → palace → old-city food walk.  
**Day 2:** Khijadiya Bird Sanctuary → local markets.
