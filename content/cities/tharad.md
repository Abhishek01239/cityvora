## Places to Explore
- **Nandevi (Ashapura Mataji) Temple** — important local religious site.
- **Tharad main bazaar** — local shopping and street life.
- **North Gujarat countryside** — useful for road trips.
- **Nadabet** — border tourism destination for a longer excursion.
- **Danta and Banaskantha** — can be combined with broader North Gujarat routes.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, bajra rotla, gathiya, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Main Bazaar and the bus-stand area**.

## Where to Stay
Stay around **Tharad town centre** for local access.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Simple Plan
Temple visit → market walk → local lunch → nearby North Gujarat excursion.
