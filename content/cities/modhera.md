## What to See
- **Modhera Sun Temple** — the main attraction, known for its carved architecture and Surya Kund.
- **Temple complex** — explore the Sabha Mandapa, Gudha Mandapa and reservoir.
- **Pushpavati River area** — adds to the heritage landscape.
- Combine the visit with **Patan** and **Rani Ki Vav**.

## Food & Local Eating
Modhera is a small heritage destination, so food choices are limited. Carry water and use nearby **Mehsana or Patan** for a larger choice of Gujarati meals and snacks.

## Where to Stay
Most travellers stay in **Mehsana or Patan** and visit Modhera as a day trip.

## Trip Budget
- Budget day trip: **₹800–₹1,800**
- Mid-range: **₹2,000–₹4,000**
- Food: **₹250–₹900**
- Local transport: **₹200–₹800**

Entry fees and transport costs can change.

## Simple Plan
**Morning:** Modhera Sun Temple.  
**Afternoon:** Patan/Rani Ki Vav.  
**Evening:** return to Mehsana or Patan.
