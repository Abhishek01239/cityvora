Anjar is a historic town in Kutch with old temples, traditional neighbourhoods and access to Gandhidham, Kandla and Bhuj.

## Places to See

Bhadreshwar Mahadev Temple area, old Anjar gates, MacMurdo's Bungalow and nearby Kutch craft centres.

## Food to Try

Kutchi food and snacks around the main market; try dabeli, bajra rotla, kadhi, sev-tameta and Kutchi thali.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=anjar%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Town centre is convenient; Gandhidham offers a larger choice of hotels nearby.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=anjar%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹900–1,600/day; mid-range ₹1,800–3,200/day; food ₹250–650/day; local transport ₹150–400/day; hotels ₹800–3,500/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
