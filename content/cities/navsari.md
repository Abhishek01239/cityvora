Navsari is a historic south Gujarat city with strong Parsi heritage and easy access to the coast. It is also a practical base for visiting Dandi and nearby southern Gujarat attractions.

## What to See
- **Parsi heritage areas** — Navsari has a long association with India's Parsi community.
- **Dandi** — the historic Salt March destination on the nearby coast.
- **Unai** — known for its hot-water springs and temple, suitable as a regional day trip.

The Navsari district administration identifies Dandi as a major heritage attraction associated with the Salt Satyagraha. citeturn1search0

## Food
Explore **Station Road, Tower Road and Dudhiya Talav** for local snacks and meals. Try **Gujarati thali, locho, khaman, ghari and Parsi-influenced local dishes**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** Station Road
- **Mid-range:** central Navsari
- **Higher-end:** hotels toward the main highway

**Typical budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500/day budget; ₹2,500–₹5,000/day mid-range. Prices vary by season and date.
