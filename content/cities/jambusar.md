Jambusar is a town and municipality in Bharuch district on the coastal plain near the Mahi River and the Gulf of Khambhat. The name is linked to an older form Jambusara. It is a practical agricultural and market town with several important religious sites in the surrounding taluka, including the well-known tidal temple at Kavi.

## Places to Explore

- **Stambheshwar Mahadev Temple (Kavi / Kamboi)** — a Shiva temple on the Mahi riverbank near the sea, known for the linga being covered at high tide and visible at low tide. About 15–20 km from Jambusar town; time the visit with the tide.
- **Local temples and bethaks** — including Pushti-marg related sites such as Harirayaji Mahaprabhuji Bethak at Bhankhetar and Acharya Mahaprabhuji Bethak at Dabha, both a short distance from town.
- **Nageshwar Talav** — the central lake in town, linked in local accounts to the name of the place.
- **Gandhi Ashram** — a modest site associated with the freedom movement and local Satyagraha participation.
- **Kavi village** — also has a Jain temple (Sasu-Vahu Na Derasar) and the coastal setting of the Stambheshwar complex.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town bazaar and lake-side area:** Gujarati snacks (fafda, jalebi, ganthiya), tea and simple thali meals.
- **Local eateries:** basic vegetarian options typical of the Bharuch coastal plain.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Jambusar town:** a limited number of modest hotels and lodges for short stays.
- **Bharuch or Ankleshwar:** larger nearby centres with more hotel choice.
- **Vadodara:** a convenient larger base if combining with city attractions.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹550/day; private vehicle is useful for the Kavi temple and tide timing.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Walk the town centre and Nageshwar Talav → local temples or bethaks → evening food stop.

**Day 2:** Timed visit to Stambheshwar Mahadev at Kavi (low tide) → return via Jambusar or continue toward Bharuch / Vadodara.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable; check tide tables carefully for Stambheshwar.
- The coastal plain can feel humid; carry water.
- Distances to the main temple are short but road conditions vary.
- Confirm current temple access and any local festival timings.
