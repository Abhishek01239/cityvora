## Places to Explore
- **Dwarkadhish Temple** — the city's central pilgrimage landmark.
- **Gomti Ghat** — riverside area near the temple.
- **Sudama Setu** — pedestrian bridge and viewpoint.
- **Rukmini Devi Temple** — important nearby temple.
- **Shivrajpur Beach** — coastal day trip option.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, farsan, khichdi-kadhi and simple vegetarian meals around **Temple Road, Gomti Ghat and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Temple Road, Dwarka town centre and near the railway station** are convenient.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹600/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Dwarkadhish Temple → Gomti Ghat → Sudama Setu.  
**Day 2:** Rukmini Temple → Shivrajpur Beach → local market.
