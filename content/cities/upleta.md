## Places to Explore
- **Taluka School heritage building** — recognizable local architecture.
- **Upleta old market** — local shopping and food.
- **Dhoraji** — nearby heritage excursion.
- **Porbandar** — useful longer day trip toward the coast.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Kathiyawadi food, Gujarati thali, gathiya and fafda-jalebi around **Station Road, Bus Stand and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Central Upleta** is practical for a short stay.
[Search Upleta hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Upleta%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Simple Plan
Old market → local food → Dhoraji or Porbandar excursion.
