## Places to Explore
- **Shatrunjaya Hill** — the defining pilgrimage landscape of Palitana.
- **Palitana Jain Temples** — one of Gujarat's major Jain heritage destinations.
- **Adinath Temple** — a key temple on the hill.
- **Palitana old town** — markets and local food.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khichdi-kadhi, farsan, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Temple Road, Taleti and the central market**.

## Where to Stay
**Taleti, Temple Road and central Palitana** are convenient.
[Search Palitana hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Palitana%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Shatrunjaya Hill temple climb → Palitana market.  
**Day 2:** Taleti → old city → nearby Bhavnagar excursion.
