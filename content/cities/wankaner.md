## Places to Explore
- **Wankaner Palace** — principal heritage attraction.
- **Royal heritage areas** — explore the city's former princely-state character.
- **Machchhu River region** — useful for the surrounding landscape.
- **Morbi** — easy nearby excursion for bridges and palaces.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Main Bazaar, Station Road and the town centre**.

## Where to Stay
**Wankaner town centre** works for a short stay; Morbi has more options.
[Search Wankaner hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Wankaner%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Wankaner Palace → town market → food.  
**Day 2:** Morbi excursion → return to Wankaner.
