## Places to Explore
- **Akshygadh Mandir** — local religious landmark.
- **Keshod markets** — food, shopping and everyday city life.
- **Junagadh** — practical nearby heritage excursion.
- **Gir region** — useful base for wider Saurashtra nature trips.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Station Road, Bus Stand and central Keshod**.

## Where to Stay
**Keshod centre and Station Road** are convenient.
