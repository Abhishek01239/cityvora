## Places to Explore
- **Borsad Stepwell** — local heritage landmark.
- **Sun Temple** — notable historic religious architecture.
- **Anand and Vallabh Vidyanagar** — easy nearby excursions.
- **Karamsad** — heritage destination linked to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Borsad market, Station Road and Anand Road**.

## Where to Stay
For the widest hotel choice, stay in **Borsad or nearby Anand**.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Borsad Stepwell → Sun Temple → market.  
**Day 2:** Karamsad → Anand → Vallabh Vidyanagar.
