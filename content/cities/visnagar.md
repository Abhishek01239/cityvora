Visnagar is a historic north Gujarat municipality in Mehsana district, known for its old urban fabric, markets and nearby heritage destinations.

## What to See
- **Visnagar old city** — explore historic streets and local markets.
- **Vadnagar** — nearby for Kirti Toran, Hatkeshwar Temple and Sharmistha Lake.
- **Modhera** — a convenient regional heritage excursion.

## Food
Try **fafda-jalebi, khaman, Gujarati thali and local farsan** around the **main market and station road**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** central market
- **Mid-range:** main road
- **Higher-end:** Mehsana provides more hotel options

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,300/day budget; ₹2,300–₹4,500/day mid-range.
