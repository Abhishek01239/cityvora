Goa combines beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, coastal villages, seafood and a wide choice of stays. Pick North or South Goa based on the pace you want.

## Places to Explore

- **Baga / Calangute:** Lively North Goa beaches, restaurants and nightlife.
- **Anjuna & Vagator:** Beaches, cafés and sunset viewpoints.
- **Panaji:** Heritage streets, riverfront areas and local restaurants.
- **Old Goa:** Major historic churches and heritage sites.
- **Palolem:** Scenic South Goa beach with a slower atmosphere.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Mapusa Market:** Local market for Goan snacks, produce and regional food products.
- **Panjim / Fontainhas:** Restaurants and cafés around the Latin Quarter; try Goan dishes and bakeries.
- **Calangute–Baga:** Dense choice of seafood restaurants and casual food spots.
- **Margao Market:** Useful for local sweets, snacks and traditional ingredients.

Individual stalls and opening hours change seasonally, so verify locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Baga/Calangute:** Lively and convenient for nightlife and North Goa.
- **Anjuna/Vagator:** Cafés, beach access and a younger atmosphere.
- **Panaji:** Useful for heritage sightseeing and central access.
- **Palolem/Patnem:** Better suited to a quieter South Goa trip.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500–₹7,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹8,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹400–₹1,800/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹800/day depending on rental/taxi/public transport.

Beachfront hotels and peak-season dates can cost considerably more. Check live prices before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Panaji → Fontainhas → Old Goa → North Goa sunset → local dinner.

**Day 2:** Choose Baga/Anjuna/Vagator for North Goa or Palolem/Patnem for South Goa → beach time → seafood dinner.

## Practical Tips

Distances can take longer than they appear on a map. If renting a scooter, follow licensing, helmet and road-safety requirements. Check weather and local beach conditions before outdoor activities.
