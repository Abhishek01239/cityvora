Goa is more than a beach destination: its coastal villages, Portuguese-influenced heritage, local food and varied landscapes make it easy to build different kinds of trips.

## Places to explore

### North Goa

North Goa has a lively mix of beaches, restaurants, markets and nightlife. Choose a base according to whether you want a busier or quieter stay.

### South Goa

South Goa is often associated with a slower pace and long beach days. It suits travelers looking for more relaxed coastal time.

### Panaji and Old Goa

Panaji and nearby heritage areas provide a useful change of pace from the beaches, with historic streets, churches and local cafés.

## What to eat

Goan cuisine includes seafood, rice-based dishes, coconut-rich preparations and distinctive Portuguese-influenced recipes. Local bakeries and casual restaurants are worth exploring alongside destination dining.

## Practical tips

Distances can look short on a map but take longer during busy periods. A scooter is common for independent exploration where legally permitted and safely operated; otherwise use taxis or other local transport.
