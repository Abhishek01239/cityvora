Mangrol is a historic coastal town in Junagadh district with a maritime character and access to the Saurashtra coast.

## Places to See

Mangrol waterfront, old town, local shrines and nearby coastal villages; Junagadh and Somnath are useful longer day trips.

## Food to Try

Local seafood and Gujarati snacks around the market and waterfront; try fish preparations where available, plus fafda-jalebi and sev-khamani.

## Where to Stay

Town centre and waterfront-side areas are the simplest bases.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=mangrol%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,500/day; mid-range ₹1,700–2,800/day; food ₹250–650/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
