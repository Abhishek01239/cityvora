## Places to Explore
- **Brahma Kund** — historic water structure and heritage site.
- **Gautameshwar Mahadev Temple** — important local temple complex.
- **Sihor hills** — surrounding landscape for short outings.
- **Bhavnagar** — nearby city for a broader trip.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi food, gathiya, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Main Bazaar, Station Road and temple areas**.

## Where to Stay
**Sihor centre** is convenient; Bhavnagar offers more hotel options.

## Simple Plan
Brahma Kund → temple visit → local lunch → Bhavnagar excursion.
