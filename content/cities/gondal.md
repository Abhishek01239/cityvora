## Places to Explore
- **Naulakha Palace** — signature royal heritage attraction.
- **Riverside Palace** — another important Gondal palace.
- **Swaminarayan Temple** — major local religious landmark.
- **Clock Tower** — recognizable city landmark.
- **Gondal markets** — useful for local textiles and snacks.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Kathiyawadi dishes, Gujarati thali, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Gondal market, Station Road and Bus Stand area**.

## Where to Stay
**Central Gondal and Station Road** are convenient; Rajkot offers more hotel choices nearby.
[Search Gondal hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Gondal%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Naulakha Palace → old city → food walk.  
**Day 2:** temple → palace area → Rajkot excursion.
