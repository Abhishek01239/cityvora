## Places to Explore
- **Amreli Tower** — recognizable local landmark.
- **Gohilwad Timbo** — archaeological site west of Amreli.
- **Jivraj Mehta heritage sites** — linked to the city's history.
- **Gir and coastal Saurashtra** — useful nearby travel region.
- **Khijadiya-style bird and nature trips** can be combined with longer Saurashtra routes.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi food, gathiya, fafda-jalebi and local sweets around **Station Road, Chital Road and the central market**.

## Where to Stay
**Station Road and central Amreli** are practical bases.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Amreli Tower → heritage walk → local food.  
**Day 2:** Gohilwad Timbo → nearby Saurashtra excursion.
