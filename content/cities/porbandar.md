## Places to Explore
- **Kirti Mandir** — memorial associated with Mahatma Gandhi's birthplace.
- **Sudama Mandir** — temple in the historic city area.
- **Porbandar Beach** — simple coastal stop.
- **Bharat Mandir** — cultural and religious attraction.
- **Chowpatty area** — useful for evening walks.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi and seafood where preferred around **M.G. Road, Sudama Chowk and Chowpatty**.

## Where to Stay
Stay around **M.G. Road, Sudama Chowk or the waterfront**.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Kirti Mandir → Sudama Mandir → old-city food.  
**Day 2:** beach → local markets → nearby coastal sights.
