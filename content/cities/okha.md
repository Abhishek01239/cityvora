Okha is a port town at the north-western tip of the Okhamandal peninsula in Devbhumi Dwarka district. It handles coastal cargo, salt and fishing boats, and it is the mainland ferry point for Bet Dwarka (Beyt Dwarka). Dwarka town and the main temple sit about 30 km south; most pilgrims sleep there and use Okha as the jetty for the island. The town itself is a working port with a lighthouse, not a beach resort.

## Places to Explore

- **Okha jetty and Bet Dwarka ferry** — the practical reason to come. Boat timings shift with tide, weather and crowd. Buy the current ticket at the jetty and do not rely on a last-season schedule.
- **Okha lighthouse** — the port landmark. Exterior views are the reliable visit; climbing or entry depends on the current port and coast-guard rules.
- **Port edge and salt pans** — a working waterfront. Stay on public roads and do not enter cargo or fishing yards.
- **Dwarka** — the temple town to the south, the usual overnight base and the railhead for the peninsula.
- **Shivrajpur and the wider Okhamandal coast** — possible add-ons only with a vehicle. They are not a walk from the Okha jetty.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Jetty and station-side stalls:** tea, bhajia, simple Gujarati snacks and thali meals for ferry passengers.
- **Town market:** everyday Kathiawadi vegetarian food. Some coastal stalls sell fish when the boats are in; availability is uneven.
- **Dwarka:** more restaurants and pilgrim meals if you are based at the temple town.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Okha:** a small set of lodges near the port and station. Useful if you have an early ferry, thin on choice.
- **Dwarka:** the practical hotel base for the peninsula, with more rooms in the October–March pilgrim season.
- **Jamnagar:** a city stop only if you are arriving by a longer train and continuing by road.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,100–₹2,400
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,600–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+ if you stay in Dwarka and add a hired car
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day.
- **Local transport:** the Bet Dwarka ferry is a modest ticket. A cab between Dwarka and Okha is often ₹800–₹1,800 one way depending on season and wait time at the jetty.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Dwarka temple and town → afternoon road to Okha → lighthouse and jetty → ferry to Bet Dwarka if the last boat still fits a return → overnight in Okha or back in Dwarka.

**Day 2:** Early ferry if you missed the island → return to Dwarka → train or road toward Jamnagar. Do not plan a same-day Rann or Gir drive from Okha.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season. Monsoon sailings are more often disrupted, and summer afternoons on the jetty are harsh.
- Ferry queues grow on weekends and festival days. Carry the island visit as a half-day, with a buffer for the return boat.
- The port is an industrial waterfront. Follow jetty staff on where passengers may wait.
- Dwarka temple rules on footwear, cameras and clothing are separate from the Okha jetty. Check both.
