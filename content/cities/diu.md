Diu is a small island town off the southern Saurashtra coast, administered as a union territory rather than as part of Gujarat. Portuguese-era fort walls, a walkable old town and short beaches make it an easy two-day add-on after Veraval or Somnath.

## Places to Explore

- **Diu Fort** — sea-facing fort at the eastern tip of the island; allow time for the ramparts and the lighthouse area.
- **St. Paul's Church** — 17th-century church in the old town, usually paired with the fort walk.
- **Naida Caves** — shell-rock cuttings near the fort, a short stop rather than a full outing.
- **Nagoa Beach** — the main swimming beach on the island; check local conditions before entering the water.
- **Ghoghla Beach** — mainland-side beach just across the bridge, useful if you are staying outside the fort area.
- **Old Diu market** — lanes around the fort for a short evening walk.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Fort / old town:** Seafood restaurants and simple Gujarati meals; ask what was landed that morning.
- **Nagoa:** Beach shacks and hotels, busier on weekends.
- **Ghoghla:** Practical restaurants if you are staying on the mainland side of the creek.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally. Alcohol rules differ from neighbouring Gujarat; follow current local regulations.

## Where to Stay

- **Near Diu Fort / old town:** Best if you want to walk to the fort and churches.
- **Nagoa:** Beach hotels, better if swimming is the priority.
- **Ghoghla:** Often slightly easier rates, a short ride across to the island.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,800–₹5,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹400–₹1,500/day, higher if you eat seafood daily.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹800/day using autos and short cab hops around the island.

Hotel prices rise on long weekends; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Diu Fort → St. Paul's Church → Naida Caves → sunset at Nagoa.

**Day 2:** Morning beach → old-town walk → optional road trip toward Somnath if you are continuing into Gujarat.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable; monsoon seas can be rough.
- Diu is a union territory. Carry ID at hotels, and do not assume Gujarat dry-state rules apply.
- Renting a scooter is common; wear a helmet and check the vehicle.
- Confirm fort opening hours, which can change.
