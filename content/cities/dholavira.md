Dholavira is a village on Khadir Bet in the Great Rann of Kutch, in Bhachau taluka, known for the Harappan city inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021. The excavated town is famous for its citadel, reservoirs and stone water channels rather than for markets or nightlife.

## Places to Explore

- **Dholavira archaeological site** — a Harappan city with a citadel, middle town and lower town, occupied from roughly the 3rd millennium BCE. Allow half a day.
- **East and north gates** — massive stone entrances; the site is known for a signboard of Indus signs found near the northern gateway.
- **Reservoirs and channels** — the water system is the clearest reason to visit, with rock-cut tanks and storm drains around the citadel.
- **Site museum** — beads, pottery and finds from the excavations, if it is open on the day you visit.
- **Khadir Bet and the Rann road** — the white salt flats on the approach from Rapar are part of the journey, especially after the monsoon when water remains in the Rann.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Village and site-approach stalls:** tea, biscuits, simple snacks and packed meals. Choice is limited.
- **Rapar or Bhachau:** the last reliable towns for a thali, dabeli or dhaba meal before the site road.
- **Carry water and a backup snack.** Do not count on restaurants inside the archaeological area.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Dholavira village:** a small number of tourist stays and a tent or resort option in season. Book ahead in winter; do not assume a walk-in room.
- **Rapar:** basic lodges if you want a shorter morning drive.
- **Bhuj or Bhachau:** more dependable hotels, with a long day trip to the site.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000–₹6,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹7,000+ (mostly the seasonal stays near the site, or a Bhuj hotel plus a private car)
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹900/day; less if you carry food from Bhuj.
- **Local transport:** the main cost. A shared or private vehicle from Bhachau or Bhuj is usually ₹1,500–₹4,000 for the round trip depending on season and group size. Site tickets are extra.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Drive from Bhuj or Bhachau via Rapar onto Khadir Bet → east gate, citadel and reservoirs → museum if open → overnight near Dholavira or return as far as Rapar.

**Day 2:** Morning light on the Rann approach → a second pass of the water structures → drive back to Bhuj, with a food stop in Rapar or Bhachau.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the practical season. Summer heat on the Rann road is severe, and monsoon roads can be slow.
- There is no city transport at the site. Arrange a car before you leave Bhuj or Bhachau.
- Check Archaeological Survey of India opening days and ticket rules. The site is closed one weekday; confirm which day.
- Footwear with grip helps on stone channels. Carry water; shade is limited.
