## Places to Explore
- **Akshardham Temple** — major cultural and architectural attraction.
- **Adalaj Stepwell** — historic stepwell a short drive from the capital.
- **Indroda Nature Park** — useful for families and nature-focused visits.
- **Dandi Kutir** — museum dedicated to the life and legacy of Mahatma Gandhi.
- **Sector 17** — central shopping and city-life area.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi and local snacks around **Sector 21, Sector 17 and Infocity**. For a wider restaurant choice, the Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar corridor has many options.

## Where to Stay
Stay around **Sector 11, Sector 16, Sector 21 or Infocity** for convenient access to the city.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

Prices vary by dates and season.

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Akshardham → Sector 17 → Dandi Kutir.  
**Day 2:** Adalaj Stepwell → Indroda → local food and shopping.
