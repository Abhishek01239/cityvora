Vadnagar is one of Gujarat's historic towns, with a long archaeological and cultural record. Its signature landmark is **Kirti Toran**, the pair of richly carved 12th-century gateways beside Sharmistha Lake. citeturn1search17turn1search3

## What to See
- **Kirti Toran** — Vadnagar's defining historic landmark.
- **Hatkeshwar Temple** — an important historic Shiva temple.
- **Sharmistha Lake** — a historic water body and city landmark.
- **Vadnagar Museum** — for the area's archaeological heritage.

## Food
Explore **main bazaar and station-area eateries** for **fafda-jalebi, khaman, ganthiya and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** central Vadnagar
- **Mid-range:** main road
- **Higher-end:** Mehsana offers more hotel choice

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,500/day budget; ₹2,500–₹5,000/day mid-range. Attraction timings and charges can change.
