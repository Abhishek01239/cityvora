Limbdi is a town and taluka headquarters in Surendranagar district, Gujarat, on the route between Ahmedabad and the Saurashtra interior. Since the opening of the BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in 2010 it has become a recognised pilgrimage stop for many devotees, while remaining a compact working town with ashrams and local markets.

## Places to Explore

- **BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir** — the principal modern landmark and the main reason many visitors stop in Limbdi.
- **Shri Jagdish Ashram** — a local spiritual centre associated with Jagannath tradition.
- **Shree Kabir Ashram and related sites** — part of the town’s quieter spiritual landscape.
- **Raj Rajeshwar Dham (Jakhan area)** — a larger temple complex a short distance east of Limbdi, useful if you have extra time.
- **Town centre** — a practical walk for markets and a sense of everyday Surendranagar-district life.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Market and roadside stalls:** fafda-jalebi, dhokla, thepla, ganthiya and chaat.
- **Local thali restaurants:** standard Gujarati combinations of dal, kadhi, vegetables and breads; undhiyu in season.
- **Pilgrim-oriented eateries:** simple satvik or pure-vegetarian meals near the main temple area.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Limbdi town:** modest hotels and lodges, including options oriented toward pilgrims.
- **Surendranagar:** a larger nearby base with more hotel variety.
- **Ahmedabad:** comfortable if Limbdi is a day stop on a longer Gujarat itinerary (roughly 100 km).

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+ (often Ahmedabad or a larger town hotel)
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹500/day; inter-city buses or cabs to Ahmedabad or Surendranagar cost extra.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** BAPS Swaminarayan Mandir → town market and local lunch → evening at a quieter ashram or central walk.

**Day 2:** Raj Rajeshwar Dham or additional ashrams → continue toward Surendranagar, the Little Rann of Kutch, or back toward Ahmedabad.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable period for outdoor time.
- Dress modestly and follow temple etiquette at BAPS and other religious sites; photography rules can vary.
- Limbdi works well as a half-day or overnight stop rather than a multi-day base.
- If combining with the Little Rann of Kutch, plan wildlife timings and permits separately.
