Deesa is a north Gujarat city in Banaskantha district and an important local commercial centre. Its location makes it useful for exploring the wider Banaskantha region.

## What to See
- **Deesa and the Banas region** — explore local markets and regional landscapes.
- **Palanpur** — a nearby heritage-oriented city and gateway to Mount Abu.
- **Dantiwada** — a nearby reservoir area for a quieter outing.

## Food
Check **Deesa Station Road and main market areas** for Gujarati snacks, thali and quick meals. Try **fafda-jalebi, khaman, sev-tameta and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** railway-station/main-market area
- **Mid-range:** central Deesa
- **Higher-end:** Palanpur for more hotel choice

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200/day budget; ₹2,200–₹4,500/day mid-range. Verify current hotel prices before booking.
