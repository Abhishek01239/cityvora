Delhi combines layers of imperial and modern history with lively markets, diverse neighborhoods and one of India's broadest food scenes.

## Places to explore

### Red Fort

The Red Fort anchors the historic heart of Old Delhi and provides context for the city's Mughal-era history.

### Humayun's Tomb

Humayun's Tomb is a major Mughal-era garden tomb and an important stop for visitors interested in Delhi's architecture and heritage.

### India Gate and central Delhi

The India Gate area works well as part of a wider central Delhi itinerary, with several major avenues and public spaces nearby.

## Old Delhi

Explore the lanes around Chandni Chowk for markets, traditional shops and long-established food spots. The area is busy, so comfortable walking shoes and a flexible schedule help.

## What to eat

Delhi is known for chaat, kebabs, parathas, butter chicken and a wide range of regional Indian cuisines. Sample both street-food classics and neighborhood restaurants.

## Practical tips

Delhi's summer heat can be intense. For sightseeing, cooler months and early starts are generally more comfortable. Check opening hours and local transport conditions before setting out.
