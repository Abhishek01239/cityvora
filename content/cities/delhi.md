Delhi is a city of Mughal heritage, colonial-era New Delhi, busy markets and a huge mix of regional Indian food. For a short trip, combine Old Delhi with central New Delhi and one South Delhi stop.

## Must-see places

- **Red Fort** — start here for Old Delhi's Mughal history.
- **Humayun's Tomb** — a major Mughal garden-tomb complex.
- **India Gate** — an easy central-Delhi stop, especially around sunset.
- **Qutub Minar** — a major historic complex in South Delhi.
- **Chandni Chowk** — go for old-market atmosphere, shopping and food.

## Street Food & Stall Areas

### Chandni Chowk / Old Delhi

**Where:** Around the lanes of Chandni Chowk, near Jama Masjid and the Red Fort area.

**Try:** Parathas, chaat, jalebi, kebabs and Old Delhi-style snacks.

This is the main food walk to build into a first Delhi visit. Individual vendors can move or change, so verify the stall's current location when you arrive.

### Bengali Market

**Where:** Near Mandi House / central New Delhi.

**Try:** Chaat, sweets and North Indian snacks.

### Safdarjung Development Area Market

**Where:** C-21, Block C 5, Hauz Khas Enclave, Hauz Khas.

## Trip Budget

Approximate planning ranges for **one person per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000 — hostel/budget room share, local food and Metro/bus travel.
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500–₹7,000 — comfortable hotel share, mixed dining and regular local transport.
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹8,000+ — higher-end hotel, restaurants and more taxis/private transport.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Red Fort → Chandni Chowk food walk → Jama Masjid area → India Gate in the evening.

**Day 2:** Humayun's Tomb → Qutub Minar → Hauz Khas/Safdarjung area → dinner in South or central Delhi.

## Quick Tips

- Use the **Delhi Metro** for long cross-city journeys whenever practical.
- Start outdoor sightseeing early in hot months.
- Keep valuables secure in very crowded market lanes.
- For street food, prefer busy vendors with high turnover and freshly prepared food.
- Check attraction timings and entry rules before leaving.
