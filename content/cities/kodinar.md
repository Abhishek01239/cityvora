Kodinar is a municipal town and taluka headquarters in Gir Somnath district, on the coastal plain east of Veraval and Somnath. The town is an agricultural market — groundnut, sugarcane and coastal coconut — more than a resort. The reason most travellers stop is Muldwarka, also called Mul Dwarka, a coastal settlement in the taluka. Local tradition treats it as an older Dwarka linked to Krishna, separate from Dwarka on the Okhamandal coast. Gir National Park is nearby in landscape terms, but official safari gates are organised from Sasan Gir and other designated points, not from a ticket window in the Kodinar bazaar.

## Places to Explore

- **Muldwarka (Mul Dwarka)** — the coastal village and temple associated with the older-Dwarka tradition. It is a short drive from Kodinar town. Treat the shore as a working coast, not a managed beach.
- **Kodinar town market** — the practical bazaar for provisions, tea and a break on the coastal highway.
- **Coastal villages of the taluka** — fishing and farm settlements. Visit only along public roads; do not assume beach access at every hamlet.
- **Somnath and Veraval** — the main pilgrimage and rail base to the west, roughly 35–45 km by road.
- **Gir forest edge** — visible on the inland side of the taluka. Safari permits, zones and timings are a separate booking. Do not drive forest tracks without a permit.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market:** Kathiawadi vegetarian meals, rotla, kadhi, dal and snack stalls. This is the reliable choice.
- **Coastal settlements:** limited tea and snack stalls; some villages sell fresh fish when the catch is in. Do not count on a restaurant meal at Muldwarka after dark.
- **Veraval:** a wider range of meals if you are based at Somnath instead of Kodinar.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Kodinar:** a handful of lodges and highway stays. Book ahead in the October–March season.
- **Veraval / Somnath:** more hotels, the temple and the railway station, if Kodinar is only a daytime coast stop.
- **Sasan Gir:** the practical base if the main purpose of the trip is a Gir safari rather than Muldwarka.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,100–₹2,400
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,600–₹5,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+ if you stay in Veraval or a Gir lodge and day-trip
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹900/day.
- **Local transport:** town autos are inexpensive. A hired car for Muldwarka plus a Somnath loop is often ₹1,800–₹3,500 depending on hours.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Reach Kodinar from Veraval, Diu or Una → town market → Muldwarka in the late afternoon → overnight in Kodinar or back in Veraval.

**Day 2:** Morning coast or farm-road loop if you have a vehicle → continue west to Somnath, or north only if a Gir permit is already confirmed. Do not arrive at a Gir gate expecting a same-day safari without a booking.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season. The coastal road is exposed in April and May.
- Muldwarka is a temple and village shore. Footwear rules apply at the shrine, and swimming is not a managed activity.
- Gir permits are limited. Book the zone before you plan the night around a safari.
- Diu is a nearby add-on by road, but it is a separate union-territory stop with its own check times on busy weekends.
