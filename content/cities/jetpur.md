## Places to Explore
- **Jetpur textile-printing areas** — the city's best-known commercial craft tradition.
- **Jetpur old market** — local shopping and street food.
- **Navagadh** — nearby heritage and local area.
- **Gondal** — palace heritage day trip.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, gathiya, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Bus Stand, Station Road and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
**Central Jetpur and Station Road** are practical.
