## Places to Explore
- **Salangpur Hanuman Temple** — major pilgrimage destination near Botad.
- **Gadhada** — important Swaminarayan heritage destination nearby.
- **Botad markets** — useful for local shopping and snacks.
- **Saurashtra countryside** — good for short road trips.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, gathiya and fafda-jalebi around **Station Road, Bus Stand area and the central market**.

## Where to Stay
Stay in **Botad town** or use nearby **Gadhada/major Saurashtra towns** for more hotel choice.
[Search Botad hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Botad%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Botad market → local food → nearby heritage.  
**Day 2:** Salangpur → Gadhada.
