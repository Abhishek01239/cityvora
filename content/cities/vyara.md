Vyara is the headquarters of Tapi district and a practical base for South Gujarat's forest and tribal-craft belt. The town itself is a market stop; the reason to stay is the road access to Songadh, Unai and the Satpura edge.

## Places to Explore

- **Vyara town market** — daily bazaar for local produce, textiles and snacks.
- **Songadh** — nearby town with a historic fort hill and Gaekwad-era links, an easy half-day from Vyara.
- **Unai hot springs** — a short trip south, popular for a soak and a picnic stop; check local access rules.
- **Tapi river countryside** — fields and forest edges on the roads toward Songadh and Valod.
- **Tribal craft stalls** — look for woven and bamboo work in the weekly market rather than a single fixed museum shop.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Main bazaar:** Gujarati snacks, undhiyu in season, fafda and simple thalis.
- **Bus stand / Station Road:** Breakfast and tea before forest-side drives.
- **Songadh market:** Backup food stop on a day trip.
- **Surat:** Wider dining if you are only passing through Vyara.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Vyara town:** Basic hotels near the bus stand and market.
- **Songadh:** Limited stays if you want to be closer to the fort road.
- **Surat:** More hotel choice, about 65–80 km away, if Vyara is a day trip.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹900–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,200–₹4,200
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹900/day if you hire a cab for Songadh or Unai.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Vyara market → Songadh fort road → evening bazaar snacks.

**Day 2:** Unai hot springs → countryside drive → return via Valod or Surat.

## Before You Go

- October to March is better for forest roads; monsoon can slow the Unai approach.
- Carry cash; card acceptance is limited outside the main market.
- Confirm hot-spring access locally before you go.
- Keep a light layer for early morning drives.
