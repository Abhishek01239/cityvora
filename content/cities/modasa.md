Modasa is the principal urban centre of Aravalli district in north-east Gujarat. It is a useful base for exploring the Aravalli landscape and nearby heritage and religious sites.

## What to See
- **Aravalli hills and countryside** — scenic drives around the district.
- **Shamlaji** — an important pilgrimage destination nearby.
- **Local markets** — useful for experiencing everyday north Gujarat commerce.

## Food
Look around **Malpur Road, College Road and the central market** for Gujarati snacks and family restaurants. Try **khaman, fafda-jalebi, sev and Gujarati thali**.

## Where to Stay
- **Budget:** central market
- **Mid-range:** main-road business hotels
- **Higher-end:** limited locally; consider larger nearby centres for more choice

**Typical budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,300/day budget; ₹2,300–₹4,500/day mid-range.
