Rajula is a town and municipality in Amreli district on the Saurashtra coast of Gujarat. It sits near the Dhatarvadi river and has a mix of local temples, a historic fort presence and access to coastal and riverside spots. The town is practical rather than a major tourist circuit stop, which keeps it relatively uncrowded.

## Places to Explore

- **Dhareshwar Mahadev Temple** — a notable Shiva temple in the Rajula area.
- **Kumbhnath Sukhnath Temple** — an older Shiva temple near the town, associated with local tradition and riverside setting; fairs are held around major festivals.
- **Chachudeshwar Mahadev** — a coastal temple area where the Dhatarvadi approaches the sea, roughly 10 km from the centre.
- **Local temples and town views** — including neighbourhood shrines that form the everyday religious map of Rajula.
- **Riverside and coastal edges** — short walks along the Dhatarvadi and toward the coast for open views (check access and safety locally).

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market and street stalls:** fafda, jalebi, dhokla, thepla, chaat and simple Gujarati meals.
- **Local eateries:** thali-style plates with dal, kadhi, vegetables and rotla or roti.
- **Seasonal items:** undhiyu in winter when available; coastal influence may appear in some non-vegetarian options, but the mainstream remains Gujarati vegetarian.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Rajula town:** modest hotels and lodges for short stays.
- **Larger Amreli-district or coastal towns:** more choice if you are combining destinations.
- **Gir periphery:** an option if the national park is part of a longer Saurashtra itinerary.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,000/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹500/day inside and around town; longer coastal or Gir transfers cost more.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Town temples and market → Kumbhnath area and riverside → evening food walk in the centre.

**Day 2:** Chachudeshwar coastal temple and sea edge → return via local viewpoints → continue to another Amreli or Saurashtra stop if desired.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable season for outdoor walking and coastal air.
- Combine with Gir or other Saurashtra sites only with realistic travel times; the coast and inland parks are not adjacent.
- Dress modestly at temples and follow any local photography guidance.
- Coastal and riverside areas can be uneven — wear practical footwear.
