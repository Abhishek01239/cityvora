## Places to Explore
- **Veraval Beach** — simple coastal stop.
- **Fishing Harbour** — one of the city's defining working landscapes.
- **Somnath Temple** — major nearby pilgrimage destination.
- **Bhalka Tirth** — important religious site near Somnath.
- **Prabhas Patan** — heritage and pilgrimage area adjoining Veraval.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, farsan and seafood where preferred around **Veraval market, Somnath Road and the harbour area**.

## Where to Stay
**Somnath Road, Veraval centre and Prabhas Patan** are convenient.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Somnath → Prabhas Patan → Bhalka Tirth.  
**Day 2:** Veraval harbour → beach → local food.
