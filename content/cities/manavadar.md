Manavadar is a municipal town in Junagadh district, once the centre of a small princely state. It offers a quiet base of local temples, everyday Gujarati life and convenient access toward the Gir and coastal areas of Saurashtra.

## Places to Explore

- **Local temples** — including Hadamtali Hanuman Mandir, Swaminarayan temples, Gayatri Mandir and other community shrines.
- **Town centre and former princely-state reminders** — modest heritage traces of the old Manavadar state period.
- **Nearby countryside and riverine pockets** — quiet walks and local agricultural landscapes.
- **Day-trip potential** — convenient position for visits toward Keshod, Junagadh or the broader Gir region (confirm distances and timings).

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town restaurants and hotels:** simple Gujarati and Kathiyawadi thalis.
- **Market and street stalls:** thepla, fafda, dhokla, local sweets (including regional specialties such as kuvar-pak noted in local descriptions) and chai.
- **Eateries near the main roads and bus stand:** everyday vegetarian meals.

Confirm current options locally as names and stalls change.

## Where to Stay

- **Town centre / near the bus stand:** most practical for short stays.
- **Simple lodges on the main approach roads:** useful if combining Manavadar with nearby towns.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹5,000
- **Comfort:** ₹5,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹1,200/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹400/day using autos or shared vehicles.

Hotel rates vary by season and availability.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive and settle → Hadamtali Hanuman Mandir and other central temples → market and evening snacks.

**Day 2:** Explore remaining local temples and town centre → relaxed thali lunch → depart toward Keshod, Junagadh or the Gir region if continuing the trip.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally the most comfortable season.
- Summers in Saurashtra can be hot; plan outdoor time for mornings and evenings.
- Confirm temple opening hours on the day of visit.
- Carry water and sun protection for daytime exploration.
