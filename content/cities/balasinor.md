Balasinor is a small town in Mahisagar district, on the inland roads between the Kheda plain and the eastern Gujarat hills. It was a princely state of the Babi dynasty; the ruler used the title Nawab of Balasinor until accession in 1948. Most visitors now come for the dinosaur fossil site at Raiyoli (also spelled Rahioli), about 10 km from town. The Geological Survey of India developed the park around Late Cretaceous eggs and bones from the Lameta beds. Local publicity sometimes calls it India’s Jurassic Park; the fossils themselves are Cretaceous, not Jurassic.

## Places to Explore

- **Raiyoli Dinosaur Fossil Park** — the hatchery and fossil site outside town. See the displayed eggs and the interpretation material, and stay on marked paths. Site hours and access rules change; confirm before you leave Balasinor.
- **Balasinor town** — the old princely headquarters, with a working bazaar and remnants of the state period. Palace grounds are not a free public museum; ask before entering any private compound.
- **Town market** — practical stop for tea, snacks and provisions on a Godhra–Lunawada road trip.
- **Godhra** — the larger neighbouring town, with a rail link if you need a bigger base.
- **Lunawada** — a possible onward stop to the east. Treat it as a separate drive, not a walk from the fossil park.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market and highway stalls:** tea, fafda, ganthia and simple Gujarati thali meals for bus and car passengers.
- **Raiyoli approach:** limited snacks near the park. Carry water.
- **Godhra:** more restaurants if the Balasinor choice is thin after dark.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Balasinor:** a small set of lodges. Some stays are linked to the old palace properties; confirm the current operator and what is actually open before you rely on a heritage room.
- **Godhra:** a wider hotel range and the railway station.
- **Vadodara or Ahmedabad:** city bases only if the fossil park is a long day trip with a hired car.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,200
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,400–₹4,800
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,500+ if a palace-linked room is available and booked
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹800/day.
- **Local transport:** a round trip to Raiyoli by auto or hired car is the main extra cost, often a few hundred rupees locally, more if the car comes from Godhra or Vadodara.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Reach Balasinor by road from Vadodara, Nadiad or Godhra → town market → late afternoon only if the fossil park is still open; otherwise keep the park for the morning.

**Day 2:** Raiyoli fossil park early → return to town → continue to Godhra for the train, or east toward Lunawada. Do not plan a same-evening Champaner visit unless the vehicle and gate times are already checked.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the easier season for the exposed fossil site. Summer midday is harsh, and the monsoon can make approach tracks slow.
- The park is a protected fossil area. Do not collect fragments or step off the marked ground.
- Palace buildings are not automatically open to visitors. Ask locally rather than walking into a private compound.
- Mobile coverage is workable in town and patchier on the village approach. Download the map before you leave Godhra or Vadodara.
