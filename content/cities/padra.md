Padra is a town and municipality in Vadodara district, about 6–10 km west of Vadodara city on the urban fringe. It is known for vegetable farming, toor dal, cotton and tobacco, as well as traditional gold and silver jewellery work and leather footwear. Several industrial units operate in the wider taluka.

## Places to Explore

- **Ambaji Mata Temple** — a long-standing local shrine near the Mota Talav (large pond), with a tradition of the image appearing from the tank. The present structure is a later shikharbandhi temple.
- **Mota Talav and town centre** — the large pond and surrounding lanes form a practical walking circuit.
- **Local markets** — useful for a short look at everyday commerce, jewellery shops and footwear outlets.
- **Vegetable-growing countryside** — the surrounding farmland supplies fresh produce to Vadodara and further markets; best seen as a rural backdrop rather than a formal attraction.
- **Vadodara day links** — Laxmi Vilas Palace, Sayaji Garden and the city centre remain easy add-ons if based here.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town centre and bus-stand area:** Gujarati snacks (fafda, jalebi, ganthiya, khaman), tea and simple thali meals.
- **Local eateries:** basic vegetarian options typical of Central Gujarat towns.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Padra town:** a small number of modest hotels and lodges for short stays.
- **Vadodara:** a much wider range of hotels, guest houses and apartments, convenient if you want restaurants and city access.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,100–₹2,400
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,800–₹5,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹6,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,300/day.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹500/day; autos and local buses connect to Vadodara.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Walk the Ambaji Mata Temple and Mota Talav area → town market → evening food in the centre.

**Day 2:** Morning local exploration or short rural drive → afternoon transfer to Vadodara for palace or garden visit → return or continue onward.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable for walking.
- Padra is close enough to Vadodara that many visitors treat it as a day stop or overnight base.
- Check current temple timings and any local festivals before visiting.
- Keep comfortable footwear for town lanes.
