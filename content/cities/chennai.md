Chennai (formerly Madras) is Tamil Nadu’s coastal capital, known for ancient Dravidian temples, a long urban beachfront, classical music and dance, and strong South Indian food culture.

## Places to Explore

- **Kapaleeshwarar Temple** — 7th-century-origin Shiva temple in Mylapore with a tall colourful gopuram and tank; morning or evening visits are more comfortable.
- **Marina Beach** — one of India’s longest urban beaches; popular for walks, snacks and people-watching (swimming is not advised due to currents).
- **Fort St. George** — early British fort and museum complex in George Town.
- **San Thome Basilica** — neo-Gothic church near the coast linked to the traditional burial site of St Thomas.
- **Government Museum** or day trips south to Mahabalipuram shore temples are common add-ons.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- Mylapore and Triplicane lanes for idli, dosa, filter coffee, sambar and banana-leaf meals.
- T. Nagar / Pondy Bazaar area for shopping plus eateries.
- Marina and coastal stretches have snack stalls (sundal, coconut water) and seafood restaurants.
- Central areas such as Nungambakkam and Anna Salai offer a wider restaurant mix.

Popular spots and timings change; choose busy, well-reviewed places.

## Where to Stay

- Mylapore / Triplicane for temple and beach access.
- T. Nagar or Egmore for shopping and rail connections.
- Nungambakkam, Teynampet or southern coastal areas for more hotels and restaurants.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹4,000–₹8,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹10,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹400–₹1,800/day depending on tiffin vs restaurants.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹600/day using Metro, buses, autos or app cabs.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates. Chennai International Airport and major rail stations are well connected.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Kapaleeshwarar Temple and Mylapore walk → San Thome Basilica → Marina Beach evening stroll and snacks.

**Day 2:** Fort St. George → Government Museum or shopping in T. Nagar → optional Elliot’s Beach or departure.

## Before You Go

- November to February is the most comfortable period; summers are hot and humid.
- Dress modestly for temples; remove footwear.
- Beach swimming is discouraged; stay on the sand.
- Traffic can be heavy; use Metro or plan extra travel time.
