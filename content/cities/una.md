Una is a town in Gir Somnath district on the Saurashtra coast, useful for exploring coastal villages, temples and routes toward Diu and Gir Somnath.

## Places to See

Una town, nearby coastal routes, Gir Somnath pilgrimage circuit and Diu-side excursions.

## Food to Try

Gujarati and Kathiyawadi meals around the main market; try sev-tameta, rotla, kathiyawadi thali and local farsan.

## Where to Stay

Main market and highway-side hotels are convenient for road travellers.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=una%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,500/day; mid-range ₹1,700–2,800/day; food ₹250–600/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
