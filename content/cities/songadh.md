Songadh is a town in Tapi district, south Gujarat, below a hill fort near the Tapi river and the Ukai reservoir. The name is read locally as son (gold) plus gadh (fort). The fort is associated with Bhil chief Sonara and was taken by Pilaji Rao Gaekwad in the early 18th century, so both Bhil and Maratha layers show in the telling of the site. It is a short heritage stop between Vyara, the district headquarters, and the Ukai dam area, not a large tourist city.

## Places to Explore

- **Songadh Fort** — a hilltop fort of stone and lime mortar above the town, restored in recent years as a local viewpoint. The climb is the visit; go in the morning for clearer air over the Tapi plain.
- **Ukai reservoir edge** — the dam and lake are a separate stop a short drive away. Water levels and public access points change; use designated viewpoints rather than informal banks.
- **Town market** — the practical centre for snacks, fruit and bus connections toward Vyara and Surat.
- **Forest surroundings** — Tapi district is a tribal and forest belt. Keep to roads and marked spots.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town stalls:** bhajiya, fafda, jalebi, poha, tea and simple Gujarati snacks.
- **Thali hotels:** dal, kadhi, seasonal vegetables, rotla or chapati. South Gujarat meals are often milder than Kathiawadi plates.
- **Highway dhabas:** on the Vyara–Songadh road if you are only passing through.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Songadh town:** limited lodges. Confirm a room before arriving late.
- **Vyara:** better choice of modest hotels, about 30–40 minutes by road depending on the exact start point.
- **Surat:** the city base if this fort is one stop on a south-Gujarat route.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,400–₹4,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹5,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹900/day.
- **Local transport:** autos in town are low-cost; a cab to the fort gate plus Ukai viewpoint is often ₹800–₹1,800 for the vehicle.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Reach Songadh from Vyara or Surat → fort climb and viewpoint → evening market walk.

**Day 2:** Ukai reservoir viewpoint in the morning → return via Vyara, or continue toward Saputara and the Dangs if the hills are the longer plan.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the most comfortable season for the fort steps. Monsoon turns the hill paths slick.
- There is little shade on the climb. Carry water.
- Do not treat the Ukai banks as a swimming spot; levels and currents are not visitor-managed everywhere.
- The hero image is the Songadh fort in Tapi district. It is not the similarly named Songadh sites in other states.
