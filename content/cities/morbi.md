## Places to Explore
- **Mani Mandir** — ornate landmark associated with Morbi's royal heritage.
- **Morbi Suspension Bridge** — historic bridge and city landmark.
- **Morbi Palace and Darbargadh** — explore the city's princely-era architecture.
- **Machchhu River** — central to Morbi's landscape.
- **Ceramic industry areas** — Morbi is a major ceramics manufacturing hub.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi dishes, fafda-jalebi and local farsan around **Sanala Road, Ravapar Road and the old market**.

## Where to Stay
**Sanala Road, Ravapar Road and central Morbi** are convenient.

- Budget: **₹1,000–₹2,200/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,500–₹5,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹6,000+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,200/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Mani Mandir → palace area → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Suspension Bridge area → Machchhu River → local markets.
