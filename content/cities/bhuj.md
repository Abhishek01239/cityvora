## Places to Explore
- **Aina Mahal** — palace and museum in the old city.
- **Prag Mahal** — grand historic palace.
- **Bhujia Hill** — panoramic views.
- **Kutch Museum** — strong introduction to regional culture.
- **Hamirsar Lake** — central evening stop.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Kutchi dabeli, Gujarati thali, bajra rotla, kadhi and local sweets around **Sarpat Gate, Bhuj market and Hospital Road**.

## Where to Stay
**Bhuj city centre, Station Road and Airport Road** are useful bases.
[Search Bhuj hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Bhuj%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹1,200–₹2,500/day**
- Mid-range: **₹3,000–₹6,000/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹7,000+**
- Food: **₹300–₹1,500/day**
- Local transport: **₹150–₹700/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Aina Mahal → Prag Mahal → Kutch Museum → Hamirsar.  
**Day 2:** Bhujia Hill → craft villages or a longer Kutch excursion.
