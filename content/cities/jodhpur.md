Jodhpur, the **Blue City of Rajasthan**, grew around Mehrangarh Fort and the historic Marwar kingdom. Rajasthan Tourism identifies Mehrangarh as one of the city's major heritage attractions. citeturn0search1

## Places to Explore

- **Mehrangarh Fort & Museum** — massive hilltop fort overlooking the old city. citeturn0search1
- **Jaswant Thada** — white-marble memorial near Mehrangarh; the Mehrangarh Museum Trust lists it as open daily 09:00–17:00. citeturn0search0
- **Toorji Ka Jhalra** — historic stepwell in the old city.
- **Sardar Market & Clock Tower** — busy traditional market area.
- **Rao Jodha Desert Rock Park** — restored rocky landscape beside Mehrangarh. citeturn0search3

**Attractions map:** [Explore Jodhpur attractions](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jodhpur+Rajasthan+tourist+attractions)

## Food to Try

- **Rawat-style kachori and local farsan around the old city** — try pyaaz kachori and mirchi vada.
- **Sardar Market / Clock Tower area** — a good area for lassi, kachori and street snacks.
- **Kesar Heritage Restaurant, Mehron Ka Chowk** — local Rajasthani and Indian food with views toward the old city.
- **Golden Dreams Cafe & Restaurant, Makrana Mohalla** — near Mehrangarh and the Clock Tower, with Rajasthani and Indian dishes.

Try **mirchi vada, pyaaz kachori, makhaniya lassi, dal baati churma and Mawa kachori**.

**Food map:** [Find Jodhpur food spots](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jodhpur+Rajasthan+local+food)

## Where to Stay

- **Old City / Clock Tower** — convenient for heritage sights and markets.
- **Ratanada** — practical central area with hotels and restaurants.
- **C Road / Sardarpura** — more modern dining and shopping.

## Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹2,800
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500–₹6,500
- **Premium:** ₹8,000+
- **Food:** ₹300–₹1,200/day
- **Local transport:** ₹150–₹600/day
- **Hotels:** roughly ₹800–₹8,000+ per night depending on season and property.

Jaswant Thada currently lists domestic admission at ₹50 and international admission at ₹100; timings are 09:00–17:00. Check official attraction sites for current prices before travelling. citeturn0search0
