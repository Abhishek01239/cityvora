Somnath is a coastal pilgrimage town in Gir Somnath district known for the rebuilt Somnath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, set beside the Arabian Sea.

## Places to Explore

- **Somnath Temple** — major Shiva temple complex with darshan, aarti and evening light-and-sound show (check current timings; phones may be restricted inside).
- **Triveni Sangam** — confluence of the Hiran, Kapila and Saraswati with the sea, used for rituals and walks.
- **Bhalka Tirth** — nearby site linked to the legend of Krishna.
- **Somnath Beach** — shoreline next to the temple for sunset views.
- **Prabhas Patan Museum** — small museum covering the temple’s history (entry fees and hours vary; confirm on site).

## Food & Local Food Areas

- Areas around the temple and market lanes serve Gujarati thalis, khichdi-kadhi, thepla, fafda-jalebi and other farsan.
- Coastal spots offer fresh seafood options in season.
- Simple vegetarian eateries and stalls operate near the temple complex and bus stand.

Stall and restaurant names and timings change, so check locally.

## Where to Stay

- Near the temple complex for short walks to darshan and the beach.
- Along the bypass or Veraval road for more hotel options and parking.
- Mix of guesthouses, mid-range hotels and a few resorts.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights or long-distance travel:

- **Budget:** ₹1,200–₹2,500
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000–₹6,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹7,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹300–₹1,500/day depending on thalis vs restaurants.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹100–₹500/day using autos or shared taxis.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking. Nearest rail is Veraval Junction; nearest airport is Diu or Rajkot.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Morning temple darshan and aarti → Triveni Sangam → Bhalka Tirth → evening beach walk and light-and-sound show if running.

**Day 2:** Prabhas Patan Museum → free time for markets or beach → optional short trip toward Veraval or return travel. (Sasan Gir safari is about 40–50 km away if combining.)

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally more comfortable; summers are hot and humid.
- Dress modestly for temple visits; remove footwear and follow security rules.
- Check aarti and light-show timings; they can change.
- Book accommodation early around Mahashivratri or other festivals.
