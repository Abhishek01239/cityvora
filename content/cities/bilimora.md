Bilimora is a railway-linked town in Navsari district near the Ambika River and the south Gujarat coast, useful as a base for nearby coastal and heritage trips.

## Places to See

Ambika River area, Gandevi surroundings, nearby Unai hot springs and Dandi-side coastal routes.

## Food to Try

Gujarati snacks and meals around Station Road and Main Bazaar; try khaman, locho, fafda-jalebi and Gujarati thali.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=bilimora%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Station Road and the main market area are convenient for short stays.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=bilimora%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹800–1,400/day; mid-range ₹1,600–2,800/day; food ₹200–500/day; local transport ₹100–300/day; hotels ₹700–3,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
