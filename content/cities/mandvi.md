Mandvi is a historic coastal town in Kutch famous for its beach, shipbuilding tradition and Vijay Vilas Palace.

## Places to See

Mandvi Beach, Vijay Vilas Palace, traditional wooden dhow-building yards and the old town.

## Food to Try

Gujarati and Kutchi food around Mandvi Beach and the main bazaar; try dabeli, pakwan, Kutchi thali and local snacks.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=mandvi%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Beach road suits leisure stays; the town centre is practical for budget travellers.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=mandvi%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹1,000–1,800/day; mid-range ₹2,000–3,500/day; food ₹300–700/day; local transport ₹150–400/day; hotels ₹900–4,000/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
