## Places to Explore
- **Ranchhodrai Temple** — Dakor's main pilgrimage landmark.
- **Gomati Lake and Ghat** — central place for an evening walk.
- **Dakor old town** — temples, markets and local street life.
- **Nearby Kheda** — useful for extending the trip.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khichdi-kadhi, fafda-jalebi and farsan around **Temple Road, Gomati Ghat and the main market**.

## Where to Stay
Stay around **Temple Road and the town centre**.
[Search Dakor hotels](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Dakor%2C+Gujarat)

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Ranchhodrai Temple → Gomati Lake → market.  
**Day 2:** nearby Kheda/Anand sights → return for evening food.
