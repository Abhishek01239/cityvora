Pune is a major Maharashtrian city in the Western Ghats foothills, historically the seat of the Peshwas and today a centre for education, IT and food.

## Places to Explore

- **Shaniwar Wada** — 18th-century Peshwa fort palace with remaining walls, gates and grounds; evening light-and-sound show when running (entry fees apply; confirm timings).
- **Aga Khan Palace** — historic palace and Gandhi memorial site a short distance from the centre.
- **Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple** — popular Ganesh temple in the old city.
- **Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum** — collection of traditional artefacts and crafts.
- **Parvati Hill** — temple complex with city views; short climb.
- Day options include Sinhagad Fort (about 25 km).

## Food & Local Food Areas

- Old-city peths and FC Road / Deccan area for misal pav, vada pav, puran poli, sabudana khichdi and Maharashtrian thalis.
- Camp and Koregaon Park for a wider mix of restaurants and cafés.
- Sweet shops sell bakarwadi and other local snacks.

Specific eatery names and peak hours change; ask locally or check recent reviews.

## Where to Stay

- Shivajinagar / Deccan / Camp for central access to old-city sights.
- Koregaon Park or Kalyani Nagar for more restaurants and hotels.
- Hinjewadi or Baner areas for IT-side stays if needed.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹4,000–₹7,500
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹9,000+
- **Food:** roughly ₹350–₹1,600/day depending on street food vs restaurants.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹150–₹700/day using Metro, buses, autos or app cabs.

Hotel prices vary by date and season; check live rates. Pune Airport and major rail stations serve the city; Mumbai is about 3 hours by expressway or train.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Shaniwar Wada morning visit → nearby old-city temples and markets → Kelkar Museum → evening light-and-sound show if available.

**Day 2:** Aga Khan Palace → Parvati Hill or shopping/food walk in Camp or FC Road → optional Sinhagad half-day if energy allows.

## Before You Go

- October to March is generally pleasant for walking; monsoons can be heavy and summers hot.
- Early morning or late afternoon is cooler for outdoor sites.
- Check monument timings and show schedules on the day.
- Traffic can be dense; allow extra time between areas.
