Bhachau is a taluka town in eastern Kutch on National Highway 27, between the Samakhiyali junction and Bhuj. It was badly damaged in the 26 January 2001 earthquake and has since been rebuilt as a highway and market town, with day trips toward Rapar, Kanthkot and the eastern edge of the Rann.

## Places to Explore

- **Bhachau town gate and market** — the rebuilt town centre and a practical stop on the Ahmedabad–Bhuj road.
- **Tappar Dam** — a reservoir west of town; Narmada water has been released into it through a pumping scheme inaugurated in 2017.
- **Kanthkot Fort** — a large historic fort in the Vagad countryside of Bhachau taluka; the main gate is a state-protected monument. Allow time for a rural drive.
- **Ashapura Mata Temple** — a local temple stop in town.
- **Chobari and the Rapar road** — the usual approach toward eastern Kutch villages and, farther on, Dholavira.

## Food & Local Food Areas

- **Town market and bus-stand lanes:** tea, fafda, dabeli and simple Gujarati snacks aimed at highway travellers.
- **Highway dhabas on NH-27:** thali, kadhi-khichdi, bajra rotla and Kutchi-style meals; useful if you are driving between Gandhidham and Bhuj.
- **Local sweet and namkeen shops:** north Gujarat and Kutchi snacks for the road.

Stall names and timings can change, so check the current vendor location locally.

## Where to Stay

- **Bhachau town:** a small set of lodges and highway hotels, convenient if you are breaking a drive or leaving early for Kanthkot or Dholavira.
- **Samakhiyali:** the nearby junction has basic highway stays.
- **Gandhidham or Bhuj:** a wider hotel choice if Bhachau is only a stop.

## Trip Budget

Approximate **per person, per day**, excluding flights:

- **Budget:** ₹1,000–₹2,000
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,200–₹4,000
- **Comfort/premium:** ₹4,500+
- **Food:** roughly ₹250–₹800/day at dhabas and town stalls; restaurant meals cost more.
- **Local transport:** roughly ₹200–₹900/day. Autos cover town; a hired car is more practical for Kanthkot or Rapar.

Hotel prices vary by date, location and season; check live rates before booking.

## Two-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Arrive via NH-27 → town market and gate → Ashapura Mata Temple → Tappar Dam at sunset → dinner at a highway dhaba.

**Day 2:** Morning drive to Kanthkot Fort → return via Chobari or Rapar road villages → continue to Bhuj, Gandhidham or Dholavira.

## Before You Go

- October to March is the comfortable season; summer afternoons on the highway are harsh.
- Bhachau is a stopover town more than a sightseeing city — plan fort and dam visits with your own vehicle or a hired car.
- The 2001 earthquake is part of local memory; rebuilt neighbourhoods are ordinary working streets, not a memorial circuit.
- Carry water for rural drives toward Kanthkot and Rapar.
