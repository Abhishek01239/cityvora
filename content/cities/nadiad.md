## Places to Explore
- **Santram Mandir** — important local temple.
- **Govardhanram Smriti Mandir** — heritage and literary connection.
- **Nadiad old city** — useful for local architecture and markets.
- **Vallabh Vidyanagar and Anand** — easy nearby excursions.
- **Kheda region** — useful for short road trips.

## Food & Local Areas
Try Gujarati thali, khaman, fafda-jalebi, farsan and local sweets around **Santram Road, Station Road and the central market**.

## Where to Stay
**Station Road, Santram Road and central Nadiad** are convenient.

## Trip Budget
- Budget: **₹900–₹2,000/day**
- Mid-range: **₹2,300–₹4,500/day**
- Comfort/premium: **₹5,500+**
- Food: **₹250–₹1,100/day**
- Local transport: **₹100–₹500/day**

## Two-Day Plan
**Day 1:** Santram Mandir → old city → food walk.  
**Day 2:** Anand → Vallabh Vidyanagar → return to Nadiad.
