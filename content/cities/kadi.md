Kadi is a town in Mehsana district north of Ahmedabad, with a strong local commercial base and convenient road access across north Gujarat.

## Places to See

Kadi town centre, local temples, nearby Mehsana attractions and the wider Modhera–Patan circuit.

## Food to Try

Farsan shops and Gujarati restaurants around Main Bazaar and Station Road; try khaman, fafda-jalebi and Kathiyawadi dishes.

**Food map:** [Open nearby food options](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=kadi%20Gujarat%20food)

## Where to Stay

Main Bazaar and Station Road are practical areas for budget stays.

**Hotel search:** [Find hotels on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=kadi%20Gujarat%20hotels)

## Budget

Budget ₹700–1,300/day; mid-range ₹1,500–2,500/day; food ₹200–500/day; local transport ₹100–250/day; hotels ₹600–2,800/night.

Prices vary by season, dates and property.

## Getting Around

Use local auto-rickshaws, taxis and buses for short trips. For nearby attractions, a cab or private vehicle is usually more convenient.
