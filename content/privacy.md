# Privacy

Cityvora is designed to provide public city and travel information. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

## Contact and forms

If interactive forms or third-party services are enabled, information submitted through those services is handled according to the applicable provider's privacy policy.

## External services

Cityvora may use external services for images, analytics, hosting, or other site functionality. Those services may process technical information such as requests, browser details, or IP-related data according to their own policies.

## Updates

This page may be updated as the website and its services change.
